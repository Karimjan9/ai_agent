# Learning-cycle acceptance

The unit of progress is an exact bounded question, not a generation or lesson
count. Existing Academy, causal runner, canonical outbox and research arbiter
remain the owners; there is no second scheduler or automatic trading authority.

## Required sequence

1. Freeze family, context, baseline, intervention, data/MTF, execution and both
   evaluator identities before dispatch. An Academy successor can use an
   observed candidate as a research scaffold, not an economic parent.
2. Replay all required arms under the same contract. Keep technical failure,
   data dependencies, underpowered observations, no effect and scientific loss
   distinct. Zero trades do not establish a harmful skill.
3. Seal immutable request, response, trade ledger and complete decision trace
   before publishing completion. Deliver idempotent learning after commit.
4. Settle the exact comparison and persist a terminal answer or explicit next
   work/dependency. Observed improvement alone is not confirmation.
5. Apply only the verified exact context/family/baseline/old-new trait. A changed
   baseline requires a bounded transfer test, not a relaxed application gate.
6. Retention requires real parent, trait-bearing child and exact trait-ablation
   models, matching source/context/data and completed immutable runs. Boolean
   claims without those witnesses are not retained benefit.

## Measurements

`ExperimentQualityProgressService::snapshot` separates technically completed
experiments, answer-producing comparisons, retained benefit and confirmation
routes. Information lessons and drift rechecks are not causal skills. Worker
elapsed time is not measured CPU, tokens or financial cost.

`TypedInstrumentFoundryService` preregisters the existing causal triplet's equal
replay caps and verifies immutable fold/arm identities before reporting actual
economic replay CPU. The ordinary causal cohort additionally records
`causal_selector_observation_v1`: memory exposure, baseline/legal space,
independent blinded choice, selected delta and scoped selection wall time.
Identical blinded/guided treatments are a legitimate null comparison, not a
reason to condition the blinded choice on memory.

Selection wall time, economic replay time and end-to-end discovery time are
different measurements. A favorable replay, equal caps or five question
registrations do not prove memory superiority. Missing metrics remain unknown;
superiority and retained economic value require preregistered comparisons and
the applicable independent evidence.

## Confirmation routes

- Historical Academy/causal research can run under its existing frozen causal
  contract and safety gates. Used discovery data is not independent validation.
- Instrument/activation confirmation requires authorized post-paper research
  windows and original transport/run provenance. 2026 remains paper-only.
  Empty or future reservations are dependencies, not executed validation.
- Paper, economic-parent and inheritance authority retain separate forward,
  risk and descendant requirements. Neither research route bypasses them.

## Recovery and release

Same-generation recovery checks the original release before restoring data and
serializes its release hash into queued admission. Changed sealed source is
terminal technical history under the bounded repair owner, not a strategy loss
or permission to reseal the old experiment. A stale loaded worker is a reload
dependency. Legacy unsealed generations are not retrospectively sealed.

Deploy at a verified drain boundary; verify the source artifact, restart and
attest consumer hashes, then resume the canonical arbiter. Fixtures prove
wiring, not market edge. Runtime acceptance requires a fresh post-release exact
comparison reaching settlement and its next decision without rewriting history.
