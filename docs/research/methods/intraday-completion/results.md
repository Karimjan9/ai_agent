# Three complete intraday methods: original two-month results

2026-10-16. All 12 predeclared original archive replays completed. None of the
three treatments qualified for the predeclared provisional agent-practice
continuation. This closes a bounded discovery batch, not the larger objective
of a useful independently qualified skill and a stronger descendant.

## What actually ran

The six fixed arms ran on full June and September 2025, not selected profitable
weeks. June contains 5,989 loaded / 200 warmup / 5,789 evaluated rows;
September contains 6,241 / 200 / 6,041. Every account began at 10,000 with the
same execution contract, maximum 1% executable stop-risk, leverage ceiling 5,
UTC 07:00–14:00 entry session and six-observed-row time stop. No parameters,
deadlines or source files changed during execution. There was one attempt per
arm, no timeout, replacement, retry or hidden tail truncation.

Both months lack observed BID/ASK. Costs remain modeled: 35 spread points,
2 slippage points, point size 0.01, commission 0.01% and daily carry 0.002%.
These are reused-archive research observations, not a broker demo, untouched
validation, tick execution or an independent session qualification.

## Reusable know-how, not a global instrument verdict

**Bollinger:** this exact population-band/midline-exit bundle adds trades but
worsens cash and closed-cash drawdown in both months. It is not unactivated:
the original method schedule contains 397 and 362 calls, with 17 and 15 actual
method-owned full exits. Treatments average 4.24 and 3.59 mature closes per
eligible observed day; the frequent-entry objective is reached, profitability
is not. The earlier separately retained October result also worsened by
260.30; it is not replaced by these products. This is scoped negative evidence
about one lab bundle, not a ban on Bollinger or all author methods.

**EMA pullback:** structural protection and nominal 2R do not improve this
same-entry method in either month. Only 7 and 5 mature trades occur per arm,
below the declared 20-trade research-practice prerequisite. There are 64 and
57 raw strategy signals. Control rejects 43 and 40 flat opportunities outside
the fixed session, and 13 and 11 for confidence. This is economic loss plus
low opportunity under this declared setup, not a missing-data or zero-signal
failure under the declared modeled-cost contract. It does not meet the requested multiple-trades-per-day objective.
Session and confidence were not loosened after seeing the result.
The 07:00–14:00 session is our fixed laboratory choice, not an established
requirement of these source lessons. Rejected opportunities do not prove that
outside-session trading is worse or that widening the session would be profitable.

**Breakout–retest:** the frozen-level method genuinely activates but has only
3 and 4 mature trades. Its June relative improvement remains an absolute
loss and reverses in September. Lower closed-cash drawdown accompanies fewer
trades; it is not general dominance or a proved
exit skill. New raw signals are 53 and 66; 41 and 49 flat opportunities are
outside the session, with 8 and 13 confidence rejections. The old rolling-level
controls remain intact. This bundle changes topology, level-relative confidence
and management together, so individual entry/stop credit is unavailable.

The fee decomposition also prevents a blanket diagnosis. September EMA control
has +20.74 before explicit commission/carry but -1.63 after them; June retest
treatment has +0.50 before those fees and -7.02 after. Spread/slippage already
remain embedded in those pre-explicit-fee cash figures: neither is a zero-cost
market-edge estimate. These figures add recorded fees back along the original
account path; they are not a rerun of a no-fee account or a counterfactual
capital curve. Bollinger remains negative even before explicit fees in
both months. Reducing the test cost assumptions is not an accepted repair.

## Next decision and agent boundary

The original comparison selects no method and persists
`retain_results_no_automatic_agent_handoff`. None of the treatments is profitable
in both periods, and EMA/retest are also below the declared opportunity floor.
The small positive September retest control is nine trades, not a qualified
skill. No curator result is imported as an agent's own outcome, policy, earned
credit or stronger-child receipt. Provisional positive/negative/unknown
know-how is retained here and in the immutable originals rather than erased.

The next discriminating work is source/setup and opportunity diagnosis, not
another unchanged generation or an after-outcome parameter sweep. Preserve the
ATR controls; do not make the new structural/2R variant an automatic default.
For EMA/retest, first determine whether a complete source method actually calls
for the fixed session/confirmation combination and can produce the intended
scalp activity. Any changed clock or confirmation is a new declared discovery
question, not technical completion or independent replication. For Bollinger,
do not rerun this identical losing bundle; a different published setup needs
its own full operational rules and controls.

An actual favorable method, its fresh admitted agent use and trait-removal
comparison remain an unmet result, not merely a missing status label.
Untouched validation has not appeared. All 2026 and later market data remain
paper-only; no independent, Master, Champion, paper or live authority is created.
