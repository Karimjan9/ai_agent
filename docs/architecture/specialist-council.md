# Versioned specialist council and one execution owner

The council is a team allocating **one account**, not a vote that replaces four
trading horizons with one winner. Scalp, hour, day and swing are holding and
decision horizons, independent of the M5/H4/H1/M15 sensor timeframes.

## End-to-end ownership

1. `SpecialistCouncilContractService` seals typed member/component passports,
   native model vectors, horizon data prerequisites and versioned routing,
   allocation, risk and execution policies. Missing resolution or data is a
   dependency, not permission to fabricate an observation.
2. `SpecialistCouncilLifecycleService` stores drafts and binds an unused native
   research model to a stored council version. `LabAgentEvaluationService`
   sends that contract through the existing screening/full/batch dispatcher.
   A declared invalid binding must not fall back to an ordinary single strategy.
   `SpecialistCouncilPreparationService` prospectively assembles a complete
   research plan in an already constructor-complete unused laboratory draft;
   it does not create another population or queue owner.
3. Python's `specialist_council` engine advances specialists on one closed
   market timeline. Intents compete for shared reservations; open positions
   keep their owner, strategy, tactic and management versions. Closed positions,
   costs, cash, exposure and risk form one account ledger. Independently run
   backtest totals cannot be added to create a council result.
4. Evaluation plans seal equal capital, execution/cost policy, windows and
   candidate/champion/solo/ablation/retention arms **before** outcomes. A different
   evaluator reads original immutable requests/results. A research comparison
   is not independent qualification, paper admission or economic credit.
   Immutable run completion atomically publishes a durable evaluation delivery;
   its consumer runs after the original seal transaction. The existing
   locked arbiter retries that consumer, not the original arm. Retries are
   bounded and cannot select a later, better run to replace the first outcome.
   Assessment and its scoped knowledge publication are atomic; a publication
   failure cannot leave a falsely completed learning handoff.
5. Approval, adoption and rollback are versioned future-entry transitions.
   Native model bindings are rechecked and published transactionally. Rollback
   restores the prior approved version even when members share model IDs;
   existing orders retain the council/management version pinned at entry.
6. `PaperTradingExecutionService` remains the only paper execution owner.
   Opt-in specialist bindings still require each native member's E3/E4 identity,
   sentinel, discipline and global risk approval. `SpecialistPaperAccountService`
   locks shared integer capital/reservations before publishing orders and fills.
7. `SpecialistCouncilDataUseService` records original event exposure and matured
   feedback under the existing research/paper epoch policy. A paper close is not
   automatically research-validation permission.
   `SpecialistCouncilResearchFeedbackService` converts original comparison
   evidence into the existing scoped knowledge/work store and supplies verified
   prior observations to a later research preparation, without creating credit.

Research, trading and version-adoption clocks remain separate. The existing
ResearchLoopArbiter is still the only autonomous research selector; no council
scheduler or automatic solution Arena is introduced.

## Prospective canonical research intake

Preparation takes a complete unused canonical `draft`, a bounded manifest and
research plan, attributable different creator/evaluator identities, and one
explicit research question. It uses the dispatcher's existing lease and locks
the generation/models. All member/control/ablation models must belong to that
draft; reused or observed models, already sealed/queued drafts and another
experiment's reserved carriers are rejected. Each research window preregisters
one candidate, one solo control and at least one nonempty ablation with equal
capital, cost, risk, evaluation bounds and actual native identities. The request
admission owner checks every arm before the preparation transaction commits.

The receipt is `prepared_for_canonical_dispatch`, not executed or qualified.
Snapshot/MTF/release admission and ordinary evaluator jobs still run through the
canonical lab dispatcher and existing arbiter. Research-purpose arms retain
original screening evidence, including economic diagnostic reasons, while
`SPECIALIST_COUNCIL_RESEARCH_ONLY` refuses ordinary economic screening and full
replay selection. A discovery experiment does not need to invent an earned
champion before it can compare against its solo and ablation controls.
Independent qualification still needs its full preregistered comparisons,
authorized untouched windows, matured power, role coverage and risk guards.

Optional `discovery_bundle_manifest` preparation uses the existing clean-MTF
owner's readiness and actual stream restoration. Its original bundle/manifest
hashes, plan UTC calendar, 15000 evaluated rows and 512 warmup rows are frozen
in the preparation receipt. `inspectDiscoveryOwner` supplies one shared verified
owner to the existing dispatcher, snapshot admission and evaluator; the Academy
guard remains unchanged. `isResearchGeneration` checks every agent/model and
the original/current source before fencing the whole generation research-only,
including native member carriers outside the comparison arm roster. A declared
tampered preparation throws, not a fallback to ordinary promotion. This clean
slice cannot be upgraded into full replay or independent qualification.

## Scoped feedback and successor consumption

After the original arm set completes, the existing evaluation-delivery consumer
seals the council assessment and publishes a conversion receipt plus knowledge
through `ResearchExperimentConversionKernelService` in the same transaction.
Original plan/manifest/run/request/response/data/parameter/code hashes are
rechecked. Duplicate delivery reuses the same assessment and receipt instead of
granting another observation or credit.

The observation distinguishes `technical_unassessable`, `data_missing`,
`underpowered` and `research_compared`. A complete powered local comparison can
be promising, negative or null. Negative/null closes only that original scoped
question, not a globally forbidden instrument or strategy. A promising research
comparison leaves a blocked independent-validation dependency; missing data,
power or technical evidence leaves its own blocked dependency. These follow-ups
are explicitly non-executable until their prerequisites are satisfied. An
authorized untouched research window is not created by a plan, provider switch,
new version or a new hash. The independent follow-up retains the post-paper
research policy (2027 onward); literal 2026 remains paper-only.

A later preparation reads up to eight server-verified prior council conversion
receipts and seals their original IDs, hashes, source runs and digest in the
plan and `specialist_council_research_consumption_v1` receipt. Retry revalidates
the exact original snapshot, not today's new top-ranked observations. This is
an auditable research-question handoff; it does not assert a confirmed trait
was inherited or that parameters changed. A bounded lookup rejects a previously
completed same-release physical question across new council/model/window or
provider labels. Changed source may permit a separately preregistered repair,
but replaying the same market events still is not independent confirmation.

This path gives a negative or provisional experiment a durable consequence
without fabricating `repair_credit`, `causal_skill_credit`, qualification,
Economic Parent or trading authority. Confirmed skill and retained descendant
benefit remain separately evidenced transitions.

## Typed products and learned operators

Trading roles propose intents and manage owned positions. Strategy, tactic,
toolbox, learning and evolution roles produce typed research products, not
orders. Risk may reduce or reject; capital allocates within external limits;
execution alone reconciles an approved order. No role may edit its evaluator,
self-certify or increase external hard-risk limits.

`TypedInstrumentFoundryService::compileResearchCandidate` admits a bounded
well-typed proposal without pretending an Academy transfer already succeeded.
Its separate `decisionOperatorContract` exports a registered AST/CALL program
as `typed_operator_decision_v1`. The native engine consumes actual prior-closed
inputs for confirmation, risk reduction or exit. External expected-output
vectors are forbidden. Risk multipliers stay in `[0,1]`; unsupported temporal
operators need an explicit adapter. Exact JSON copies preserve PHP/Python
numeric hashing but must match the transported structured content.

Operator behavior and its absence/ablation are compared on the same timeline.
Invocation or an interpreter speedup is not proof of economic contribution.

## Honest boundaries

- Historical research remains pre-2026. Literal 2026 data remains paper-only.
- A fresh council version, dataset hash or provider does not create an untouched
  validation window. The authorized-window owner still verifies original
  chronology, prior exposure and completion.
- Second-scale scalp requires ticks, quote age, latency and real fill evidence;
  an M5 candle replay cannot claim that precision. Swing needs carry, rollover,
  gaps and matured holding outcomes.
  End-of-replay force closes are censored, not matured qualification samples.
  Sealed daily carry assumptions are recorded; without a broker rollover
  calendar they do not certify broker-specific swing rollover competence.
- Native research distinguishes hedging from broker netting. Paper netting or
  unsupported fill/management accounting is a blocked dependency, never an
  imaginary independently hedged position.
- Config defaults leave specialist paper execution **off**. Deploying code does
  not create qualified specialists, paper candidates or live authority.
- A losing or unactivated council is a valid scientific result when its original
  ledger and inputs are complete; it is not a confirmed improvement.

## Acceptance and progress

Required focused checks cover parallel long/short horizons and capital
conservation; duplicate intent/fill handling; external risk and cost vetoes;
operator behavior versus ablation; prospective upgrades with old management
version retention; numeric transport/tamper rejection; independent evaluator
and event-use policy; all-or-nothing canonical preparation; original feedback
publication/retry and poisoned successor-consumption rejection. A complete
research comparison without champion/retention must close honestly while
independent qualification remains withheld.

Progress distinguishes drafted, executed, research-compared, independently
qualified and active versions. Useful improvements require original matched
comparisons, independent authorized windows and retained child/ablation benefit.
More members, orders or generations alone do not demonstrate learning.

Production acceptance additionally requires a sealed source release, matching
loaded workers, additive migrations and a new original native replay receipt.
Synthetic deterministic tests prove wiring, not a live-market edge.

## Native operator entrypoint

`php artisan trading:specialist-council` supports `prepare`, `register`, `plan`,
`attach-arm`, `status`, `approve`, `schedule` and `rollback`. Manifest and
preregistration files must be bounded workspace JSON. Registration binds an
existing unused canonical laboratory carrier; it does not create a generation
or dispatch evaluator jobs outside the existing arbiter. `approve` rechecks
original evidence and native paper guards, not flags in an input file.

`prepare --generation-id=<unused-draft-id> --preparation=<workspace-json>`
assembles the complete prospective manifest/plan and research-consumption
receipt atomically. It is research-only and requires the existing canonical
constructor to have produced that draft; it cannot reuse an already attempted
generation or relabel an old solo receipt as an original native arm.

Expose requested and evaluated UTC bounds, warmup exclusions and row counts
in every benchmark receipt. Equal source hashes alone are not equal evaluation
windows. Memory-guided versus blinded comparison additionally needs the same
baseline, legal proposal space, seed/resource budget and original blinded
information boundary; one lucky candidate is not selector superiority.

The stored plan binds actual canonical execution parameters, shared-account
risk limits, capital and probe policy before the dispatcher seals its request.
Conflicting batch policies are refused. Warmup is feature-only: its last signal
cannot create the first evaluated opening. Unsealed incremental discovery uses
the existing bounded 5,000/2,000 tail policy; a sealed probe owns its exact
calendar. Missing producer scope cannot be filled in from the CSV hash later.
The old Laravel metadata cache is not reused for declared councils or their
evaluation arms; Python's full-request cache retains exact contract/window
identity and verifies original receipt bytes.
