# Integrations and runtime boundaries

Native specialist councils use the existing `/api/backtest/run-all`, `/run` and
`/portfolio` boundary. `specialist_council_runtime_v1` belongs to the individual
candidate, not unrelated batch peers. One native shared account emits
`specialist_council_receipt_v1` with pinned member/version, data, execution and
account/position ledger identities. Laravel's original request attestation
precedes immutable completion or cache/learning acceptance. Optional typed
operators execute actual closed observations, not benchmark expected outputs.
Exact numeric JSON copies are compared before canonical hashing. The native
producer receipt additionally carries exact canonical `receipt_json`;
Laravel verifies its SHA and structured copy before account/owner checks, so
PHP empty-object or float encoding cannot silently change the original seal.
Actual evaluated UTC bounds, warmup and decision-row counts must match the
preregistered arm; identical source SHA alone is not evaluation parity. Native
completion publishes its delivery in the original terminal transaction and
the existing locked arbiter retries only the downstream consumer.
The additive
paper account remains opt-in and uses the original E3/E4 intake/execution owner;
native deployment grants no paper or live authority.

`SpecialistCouncilPreparationService` binds a complete prospective research
manifest and original candidate/solo/ablation plan inside an unused canonical
draft under the existing dispatch lease; preparation does not call Python or
enqueue jobs. The ordinary dispatcher then owns data/MTF/source sealing and
each original request. Research arms retain their native evidence but cannot
become generic economic survivors or enter full selection merely because the
comparison is promising. Independent qualification remains a separate complete
original-authorized-window boundary.

An optional original council `discovery_bundle_manifest` is accepted only by
the existing MTF readiness/stream restoration owner with exact plan UTC bounds,
15000 evaluated rows and 512 warmup rows. The preparation seals its original
bundle/manifest hashes; dispatcher, snapshot admission and evaluator share
`inspectDiscoveryOwner`, alongside the unchanged Academy guard. Whole-generation
`isResearchGeneration` rechecks all native source and arm model hashes plus
current source before research-only screening/full-selection guards. A declared
invalid owner throws instead of falling through to ordinary authority. No
full replay or independent evidence is created by this clean-discovery route.

The evaluation-delivery consumer publishes original council assessment and
`specialist_council_research_feedback_v1` atomically through the existing
research conversion kernel. Receipt/knowledge closure distinguishes invalid
technical evidence, missing producer data, insufficient matured power and
local promising/negative/null comparison. Follow-ups carry explicit blocked,
non-executable dependencies; they are not requests to an unknown new executor.
The independent-validation dependency retains the post-paper authorized-data
policy, and no planned window authorizes 2026 research or fabricated credit.
Negative/null closes only the original scoped hypothesis, never a global ban.

Later preparation seals at most eight verified original feedback IDs/hashes,
source-run IDs and a digest in `specialist_council_research_consumption_v1`.
Retry rechecks that original snapshot rather than reranking on newer outcomes.
This boundary is research-question consumption, not inferred parameter mutation,
confirmed inheritance, paper or live permission. A bounded same-release
completed-question lookup compares native semantics and physical event bounds,
not fresh council/provider/hash labels; repeated events are never independent
evidence. The module dependency DAG describes prerequisites, not every reverse
receipt callback: these writes reuse the causal module's existing conversion,
knowledge and durable-work owners without introducing another scheduler.

Future native entry adoption and rollback publish versioned model bindings
only after original qualification, member seals and paper guards are rechecked.
Rollback restores the prior approved version's future-entry pointers, including
shared member models; original paper orders retain their council/management
pins through retirement or rollback. The existing E3/E4 sentinel/discipline/risk
owner and explicit specialist-paper opt-in remain mandatory.

Paper admission depends on original-source and exact-trait causal verification.
Causal discovery and Research Mentor do not require paper profitability; later
performance/Economic Parent handling consumes already sealed E4 receipts as
feedback. The module dependency DAG represents prerequisites, not every callback;
this reverse receipt flow remains owned by the existing paper/credit services.

## Laravel ↔ FastAPI

Laravel calls the local FastAPI service for backtest, replay, paper signal and
execution-contract calculations. The exact request/response authority is
`docs/ai-service-contract.md`. The generated integrations index locates known
HTTP endpoints and static client call sites; inspect source for environment URL,
timeouts, retries and error behavior.

For full replay, M5/M15 foundation partitions consume their own frozen pre-2026
execution archive (at least 2,000 train rows), while H1 keeps its 2005 start
requirement. All three chronological partitions remain disjoint; this repair
does not authorize a new independent research window or paper data reuse.

Archive-first scheduling removes live continuity/fresh-candle prerequisites
only for prospectively admitted historical research. The constructor validates
and seals its actual foundation; queue admission rechecks its identity and all
M5/H4/H1/M15 periods before replay. The 2026 canonical price snapshot remains a
separate paper artifact. Laravel/Python evaluator, risk and authority contracts
are unchanged; repeated discovery does not create independent validation.

Research replay requests also carry sealed specialist context plus one aggregate
composition execution authority binding component identities, instrument
assignment, dataset/MTF bundle, execution assumptions, symbol and timeframe.
Laravel derives assignment source components from the frozen passport and
invalidates a cached assignment if any source identity changes. Python compares
all four source components and selected keys against the sealed contract before
replay, even when the assignment's own hash is otherwise valid.
Instrument assignments also seal code-owned executable capability cards. Their
event lists are the same lists used by the Laravel activation contracts; a
card/label is not runtime activation or authority. Registry aliases and OHLC
structural proxies are reported separately from independent algorithms. The
management adapter reports its actual single-partial-runner features rather
than claiming every abstract profile feature is implemented.

`trading:reconcile-instrument-pairs` is a bounded SQL projection child selected
by the arbiter on `scheduler-critical`. It does not call Python or create a
generation. It rechecks exact run/reservation/context seals and closes only
unsettled candidate or frozen-control reference rows; only the former can
enter the existing paired-context posterior path. Reference closure grants no
credit. Historical reference-only maintenance is rate limited.
Python compiles the frozen typed nodes into a versioned executable DAG and
rejects invalid ports, unsatisfied edges or an empty strategy/tactic activation
scope before replay. The v2 graph is explicit in the sealed request; legacy v1
passports are not silently upgraded. Its tactic and typed-entry pipeline own
the final pre-risk
signal and its management adapter owns the open-position lifecycle. Python
returns a v3 trace with gate-emitted, hash-bound per-node receipts and every per-opportunity
decision ID/reason; only the inspection sample is bounded. Laravel refuses
composition credit unless the compiled program and runtime receipts are valid,
every required node was actually reached, and one decision-ID witness spans
MTF permission, risk, fill and a completed managed trade.
For a preregistered four-arm activation probe, the Python receipt also carries
a candle-derived `paired_context_id` distinct from its arm-specific
`decision_id`, a shared opportunity-universe hash and explicit no-signal count.
Laravel requires the four immutable responses, identical MTF/data/execution
identity and exact C/A/B/A+B parameter deltas before recording a science-only
conversion receipt. This boundary does not grant economic or live authority.
Execution cost/stress diagnostics derive their own child composition binding
after canonical validation. Their altered execution hash is accepted only in
that diagnostic lane and never re-seals the parent or grants promotion credit.
Exact specialist venue phases are normalized from the replay decision timestamp
on the Python side and compared with the Laravel-declared context contract.
The same boundary canonicalizes volatility labels before instrument activation
and attribution. Laravel does not promote a portfolio lane's diagnostic
transition-homework label into a live instrument predicate; an explicit sealed
specialist/capsule declaration is still enforced.
For instrument learning, Python now emits a separate exact venue-phase
trade-ledger envelope and activation key alongside the legacy session envelope.
Laravel matches candidate and control on the five exact axes before local
posterior settlement; a phase-scoped legacy receipt or mixed granularity stays
diagnostic. This does not waive independent research-window or paper-only gates.

Ordinary XAUUSD generation dispatch freezes an M5 execution stream and H4/H1/M15
context streams as one content-addressed manifest. Laravel admission, screening
and full replay preserve that manifest; Python verifies all declared paths and
SHA-256 values before compiling the closed-candle MTF stack. H1 remains only the
laboratory/storage identity for this organism. Volume-dependent replay receives
explicit `volume_available` markers in the frozen historical M5/H4/H1/M15 files
plus a provenance receipt bound to their content hashes. Laravel derives
`volume_context` from that manifest. The rolling/live volume audit is operational
evidence for its own time range and cannot attest pre-2026 history. A price-only
lane crosses this boundary as `not_requested`, not `volume_unavailable`.
New agent-owned bundles may also bind an exact-hash synchronized historical
quote sidecar before queue admission. M5 CSV columns preserve spread
availability, BID/ASK, quote time, close-time availability and age; the bundle
manifest contains the source/sidecar/decoder hashes. Python validates one
quote strictly before the close, age <=60 seconds and frozen BID equality.
Absent quotes stay unknown; the modeled execution cost is not an observation
and remains unchanged. Independently closing BID/ASK bars stay diagnostic.
Only new release/data identities can consume the sidecar; no admitted cohort
or immutable result is backfilled and no authority is earned by attachment.
The autonomous lifecycle does not own a second queue shortcut: it resumes the
same canonical generation dispatcher, and pauses fail-closed unless that
dispatcher has moved the generation through snapshot/MTF admission.

Autonomous causal confirmation crosses this boundary one fold at a time through
`/api/backtest/run-all`; each request carries all three arms and one global fold
index. Laravel owns retry/checkpoint durability in `causal_fold_receipts`.
One three-arm fold has its own bounded 720-900s Python child, 960s Laravel
transport and 1020s queue-job default; this is not the nine-fold monolithic
request. Queued jobs refresh their operational timeout when deserialized, so
an older serialized 360s cap cannot interrupt the updated child budget. A
stale lower Python timeout setting cannot undercut the declared three-arm
per-fold budget.
After the complete fold set is sealed, Laravel calls
`/api/backtest/aggregate-causal-folds`; Python validates identities, independent
windows and complete ledgers without re-reading market data. The aggregate is
then projected through the ordinary immutable evidence boundary.
If the triplet becomes terminal before all three immutable screening receipts
exist, Laravel invalidates it before any FastAPI fold call and grants no partial
causal credit.

The scheduler wrapper treats Laravel command transport success separately from
the lifecycle JSON outcome: `running`, `paused`, `blocked` and `deferred` remain deferred
research-loop decisions even when Artisan exits with code zero. Only an
achieved lifecycle transition may close successor/autonomy evidence.
Before a queued Laravel replay crosses into Python, the evidence middleware
checks the sealed release. Immutable source/data/execution drift is a terminal
pre-execution technical refusal, not a transport retry or strategy result;
same-generation open learning dispatches are closed without an API call.

## Research and paper epoch boundary

Prospective replay sends `research_release` with the generation's sealed
source, Python source, data/MTF and per-agent economic identity. The API checks
boot/current source before caches, and the child emits
`data_quality.research_release_receipt`; Laravel validates it before learning.
`/health.research_source` diagnoses loaded-code freshness but is not market
evidence. Legacy attempted generations stay unsealed (ADR-016).

Specialist scope receipts retain pre-scope strategy opportunities and exact
first-veto/all-predicate counts. Missing finite spread/ATR is an observed
liquidity-data gap, never substituted by the modeled execution-cost spread.
The proof frontier can defer an activation mutation on that prerequisite.

Historical discovery, mutation, screening, repair and causal confirmation end
before `2026-01-01T00:00:00Z`. The frozen 2026 block is prospective paper-only:
it may support forward/performance authority but cannot update research
selection, mutation or instrument posteriors.
Instrument confirmation uses separately authorized post-paper research
datasets. Laravel matches their server-owned UTC interval and dataset SHA-256
against an explicit research-validation replay manifest and the immutable
candidate/control pair before writing a sealed window receipt;
replaying one dataset under new labels is not independent evidence. No research
interval is authorized by default.

The original Laravel window owner may issue `authorized_research_transport_v1`
for a completed server-authorized full replay only. A domain-separated HMAC
uses the protected internal API key (never returned/logged), binding the
persisted release and exact complete CSV inventory/chronology. Python verifies
all actual files before cache reuse and validates consumed rows after loading.
Copied labels, duplicate stream path hiding, inline data, widened cutoffs and
literal 2026 remain rejected. No clock-dependent signature refresh alters the
experiment identity; only the HMAC is excluded from the scientific cache key
so coordinated key rotation cannot manufacture another observation. Original
window authorization/independence/credit guards remain separate (ADR-023).

`sealed_confirmation_route_v1` is frozen before new causal/Academy model
construction and in activation plans. It distinguishes historical causal
research execution from the blocked post-paper independent-authority route;
the route itself is not a dataset, proof or economic permission.
`authorized_prospective_paper_epoch_v1` supports explicitly approved later
paper periods through the existing E3/E4/order owners. The future registry is
empty by default. The original authorization/period/freeze seal is persisted
and rechecked before transport and E4, not reconstructed from outcome claims.
A period overlapping server-authorized research or original observed learning-
pair chronology is rejected. Post-paper training/selection provenance remains
a separate blocked dependency, not an accepted caller flag. Legacy 2026
contracts and archive snapshots are unchanged; no real future data or authority
is created by registering this contract (ADR-023).

The future-paper consumer additionally derives
`archive_baseline_confirmed_single_trait_paper_v1` from original server-owned
models/runs/artifacts. It accepts an unchanged modern archive baseline or one
canonical confirmed post-paper exact trait, never an unchecked training flag
or arbitrary future-tuned vector. Original source requests use the real nested
passport and PHP runtime-contract builder; the four-stream CSV inventory,
warmup chronology, signed transport/window identity, and per-arm instrument
runtime gates are checked separately from raw parameter identity. Wrapped
laboratory parameter hashes resolve through the original model seal, not a
string alias. Component-seal-changing traits and unsupported source planes
remain blocked. Later old-CSV replay cannot manufacture archive origin;
release drift requires trait revalidation. Legacy `paper_2026` is unchanged.
