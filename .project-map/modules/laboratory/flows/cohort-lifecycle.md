# Laboratory cohort lifecycle

## Trigger

A scheduled or operator-authorized research request asks the laboratory to open
a bounded cohort for a compatible market, data and control context.

## Sequence

1. Validate canonical data continuity, source/control identity and construction lease.
   When the arbiter selects a target-aligned causal lesson, the child lifecycle
   command carries an explicit `learning_confirmation` admission intent; it
   cannot silently fall back to generic new-data or empty technical recovery.
2. Allocate the 20 seats as evidence-ranked experiment blocks (exact pairs,
   causal triplets, factorial/replication/guard blocks) and persist immutable
   construction, session/venue and control context.
   In cold start, an immutable terminal source with a measured tactic veto may
   reserve one four-seat `activation_factorial` block, replacing two repair
   pairs while leaving novelty, guard and protected causal seats intact. The
   proposal retains incumbent/challenger evidence axes separately and spends
   one discovery trial per frozen hypothesis/data identity. If source evidence,
   semantic scope or two distinct legal deltas are unavailable, ordinary
   allocation continues without forcing an activation block.
3. After contextual copying, bind every composition seat's family,
   architecture and tactic to its frozen passport before control hashes are
   compiled; re-assert the same ownership at the final constructor boundary.
   A predeclared structural architecture is first resolved to its matching
   executable strategy ID, then frozen into the passport. Reject the cohort if
   any passport cannot own an executable runtime. For a causal triplet, the
   passport must match the source control family and every declared source
   architecture/tactic before any arm is created.
   Derive instrument-assignment source identities from those frozen passport
   components. A cached assignment with any stale source component is rebuilt;
   replay admission still verifies its exact assignment hash and source keys.
   For an activation block, C copies the selected source's exact parameter
   vector; A and B each change one declared gene; AB changes both. Prequeue
   admission recomputes common manifest and four distinct executable hashes,
   checks the C->A, C->B, B->AB baseline chain and refuses any drift.
4. Before queue admission, freeze the autonomous XAUUSD M5 execution stream
   together with H4/H1/M15 context. Both scheduled/operator dispatch and the
   autonomous lifecycle delegate to `trading:dispatch-lab`; no lifecycle path
   may enqueue an evaluator directly around this sealing authority. Dispatch
   screening work with this exact manifest and persist execution and decision
   receipts.
5. Move only eligible candidates into full replay/statistical/holdout evaluation.
   A causal triplet uses durable per-fold jobs and cannot settle before its
   complete fold-receipt aggregate is projected.
6. Admit qualified candidates into paper observation without granting live authority.
7. Settle every generation-owned learning projection, then close the mutable
   generation boundary as completed, screened or technical quarantine with
   provenance retained.
8. Verify that the generation itself carries creation provenance from the
   previous generation's exact completed arbiter writer decision. When the
   arbiter creates its successor, persist that creation provenance even if the
   predecessor audit failed; seal an autonomy receipt only when the predecessor
   audit and snapshot admission pass. The immutable receipt links both arbiter
   decisions and adjacent generations. Two consecutive linked receipts are the
   unattended end-to-end acceptance.

Learning admission scopes failed-job recovery debt to the current recovery
lookback plus the active lineage head. Older quarantined jobs remain visible in
the append-only failure audit but cannot freeze construction of all later
generations; current technical failures still require their bounded recovery
or terminal disposition before the next cohort is admitted.
When a frozen control's screening replay later completes, a dependent that was
quarantined before opening its own run remains failed diagnostic evidence; it
does not inherit the control's ordinary status as an independent transport
error. An unresolved control timeout retains its own one-shot recovery debt;
the dependent waits without consuming a second timeout-repair seat. A sealed
unrecoverable control contract ends that debt as terminal technical history,
not strategy evidence.
The batch dispatcher and every direct single-agent screening recovery apply
the same frozen-control admission immediately before evaluator execution.
Pending control projection releases the attempt without a verdict; an invalid
or incomplete control closes the attempt as skipped and leaves the dependent
in technical quarantine.
After a zero-pass cohort, a confirmed drift signal is not sent directly to the
`market_drift` builder. If the final report requests a data-edge audit and has
100% technical completion with zero pipeline failures, the arbiter routes
through the lifecycle owner; its autonomous audit changes the gate watermark,
then the next root builder consumes the explicit `data_edge_audit` handoff.
That handoff is single-use: the audit protocol and source generation number
must belong to the latest generation itself, and its final report must say
`data_edge_audit_completed`. A successor may retain the audit as provenance,
but cannot reuse the inherited field to open another root portfolio; a fresh
audit requires its own technically complete final report.
Without an eligible audit, the arbiter uses the normal `new_data` lane and the
builder still requires 24 genuinely new H1 candles. Either route must seal the
frozen M5/H4/H1/M15 manifest before replay dispatch. Fresh-data retries are
deduplicated below the candle threshold and become eligible at each complete
24-H1/96-M15 candle window, not on every scheduler minute.

`laboratory` → `market-data` → `laravel-python-contract` → `paper-execution` → `operations`

## Rules

- No duplicate active cohort for the same protected construction boundary.
- Data, execution, intervention and control identities must stay attestable.
- A composition passport, not a later constructor heuristic, owns its runtime
  strategy family, architecture and tactic.
- An unavailable stale causal dependency can fill a mandatory seat only as a
  frozen diagnostic control after its causal contract and promotion evidence
  are explicitly removed.
- The H1 laboratory key does not select the replay candle stream. XAUUSD
  autonomous screening/full replay must retain the generation's frozen M5
  execution plus H4/H1/M15 context manifest.
- A missing gate receipt is not a passing result.
- Failure evidence may guide diagnosis but cannot become promotion evidence.
- An old `failed_jobs` row is not live learning debt solely because its
  quarantined source agent still exists; it must belong to the current recovery
  scope. Keep the failure row and its generation evidence intact.
- A confirmed market-drift observation cannot bypass zero-pass admission. An
  eligible final-report audit uses the lifecycle owner first; otherwise use the
  normal `new_data` trigger and retain the drift receipt as the selection reason.
- If a technically complete final report explicitly requires a data-edge
  audit, only the lifecycle owner may record that non-promotional audit. Its
  report/audit disposition is part of the arbiter state watermark, allowing one
  successor selection after the deferred audit without minute-based retries.
- Inherited `data_edge_audit` context is provenance, not new admission authority;
  a mismatched source generation or unfinished report cannot select the
  `data_edge_audit` constructor lane even when the field is present.
- Static semantic groups are a fallback/safety scaffold, not the permanent seat
  constitution; all active scientific blocks retain exact control ownership.
- A challenger cannot replace the best-known contextual specialist without the
  applicable independent evidence and authority transition.
- `full_validation` cannot remain active after agents, immutable runs, queues
  and the settlement watermark are terminal. A technical child closes the
  generation honestly as technical quarantine; it never manufactures a pass.
- Operational terminality is distinct from technical integrity: a quarantined
  cohort is closed work and simultaneously a failed unattended acceptance.
- A generation admitted without its immutable M5/H4/H1/M15 bundle remains
  terminal diagnostic history. Replaying its agents cannot reconstruct the
  missing generation-time authority and must not block a fresh successor.
- A lifecycle cycle that cannot move its generation through canonical snapshot
  admission pauses before evaluator dispatch; it must not report the pass as a
  completed lifecycle advance.
- An exit-zero lifecycle response with `paused`, `blocked` or `deferred` status
  is a deferred arbiter transition, not a completed one. Arbiter protocol
  revisions fence repaired decision semantics while the state hash continues
  to suppress unchanged per-minute no-op dispatch.
- `running` with `technical_recovery` also means only a bounded repair was
  dispatched, not that the arbiter's requested successor was created. An
  unrecoverable mismatch with the prior run's dataset hash is sealed as
  terminal technical history with no replay or strategy credit; that receipt
  changes the arbiter state watermark so it may reselect the successor once.
- A batch replay interrupted after a host suspend is retry-released and queued
  against the same frozen snapshot. A normal bounded transport timeout remains
  immutable technical evidence; neither case may silently become a screen pass.
- Zero candidates or screen passes may produce
  `passed_with_scientific_abstention`; timeout, missing bundle, incomplete fold
  receipts or incomplete immutable evidence cannot.
