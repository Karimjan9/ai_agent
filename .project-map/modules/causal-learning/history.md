# Causal-learning history

## 2026-10-03 - Exact evidence scopes and canonical Academy admission

ADR-022 connects Academy draft intents to the existing arbiter/dispatcher,
replaces count-based stage proof with decision/event identity, and partitions
instrument validation by exact delta/epoch. Discovery and legacy receipts stay
research-only; paper candidate and external idea identities are rechecked at
their execution boundaries. This is not a market confirmation or new data policy.
Continuation-first selection prevents fresh discovery from starving attested
Academy/director follow-ups, while a stable-input cap bounds the cold start.
Direct settlement and continuation revalidate the original complete arm proof.

## 2026-09-29 - Executable instrument capabilities and projection closure

Capability cards share activation-event ownership, expose parameter/data/stage
requirements, and identify proxies and simplified profile engines. Discovery
prioritization consumes sealed source learnability. The arbiter closes bounded
exact-pair projection debt, including previously stranded frozen-control
reference invocations, without credit or historical terminal relabeling.
Four-arm attribution now derives local interaction/removal effects only from
complete matched receipt identities. See ADR-020.

## 2026-09-28 - Prospective promising-screen repair

A promising immutable screen pair can purchase one new exact three-arm
experiment, not retrospective credit. Instrument treatment parity, explicit
learnability states, preregistered blocked independent validation and
idempotent discovery closure preserve uncertainty and the paper-only epoch.
Generation reporting separates independent improvement, memory/blinded
diagnostics, retained benefit and worker-elapsed knowledge throughput.
See ADR-019.

## 2026-09 — Conversion-loop and composition navigation

The project is implementing a bounded causal-learning vertical slice: exact
counterfactual cohort construction, parity/contract checks, settlement,
conversion receipts and next-work routing. Project Map records its ownership
and orthogonal-state model so future changes do not reduce it to a score-only
workflow.

The detailed proposal is not copied here; consult the active blueprint and
source/tests for policy authority.

## 2026-09 - Durable causal fold execution

Autonomous nine-fold confirmation moved from one monolithic replay request to
independently retryable three-arm fold jobs. Immutable fold receipts resume
after restart, and only a complete aggregate may enter causal settlement.

## 2026-09 — Context-proof ratchet and paper epoch separation

Causal component credit now retains sealed source context and observed runtime
activation. Mentor evidence is monotonic across later economic receipts, while
Economic Parent/performance evidence is separated behind the frozen 2026 paper
epoch instead of being inferred from historical forward replay.

## 2026-09 - Instrument source identity rebind

Cached instrument assignments now derive strategy, tactic, risk and management
source IDs from the frozen passport. Any drift forces a new assignment, while
Python independently rejects a hash-valid assignment from different source
components before replay. This prevents stale invocation attribution.

## 2026-09 - Confirmed causal credit handoff

Post-v2 component confirmation now requires the lattice's risk and context
checks before sealing `confirmed`. One exact, proof-carrying triplet issues
idempotent repair and causal-skill ledger receipts together; the Research
Mentor projection follows that receipt. Credit status storage was widened so
longer existing paper and descendant status codes can persist under strict
MySQL. Historical provisional experiments remain unchanged.

## 2026-09 - Behavioral activation separated from economic credit

Four-arm activation settlement now compares paired candle opportunities and
immutable MTF/replay receipts. A joint activation closes as a semantic
research hypothesis with one durable, blocked independent-window follow-up;
the ordinary factorial effect graph and component-credit writer are bypassed.
The conversion classification explicitly distinguishes behavioral activation
from economic positive candidates. See ADR-009.

## 2026-09 - Future validation interval preregistered

Activation proposals now seal a post-paper six-window validation rule before
the four arms are built. Each arm and the conversion receipt carry its hash.
The work remains blocked pending a separately authorized research epoch and
dataset; a reserved interval never becomes paper, causal or economic credit.
See ADR-010.

## 2026-09 - Exact venue-phase instrument attribution

Prospective instrument research now pairs candidate/control trade slices and
runtime activation at the same five-axis venue-phase context. Legacy broad
session slices remain diagnostic for phase-scoped tools. Confirmed local value
also cannot steer a successor whose requested context omits that phase. No
historical receipt or missing independent-window proof is upgraded. See ADR-012.

## 2026-09 - Instrument window authority and successor use

Instrument posterior confirmation now requires server-sealed post-paper
research windows with distinct datasets and non-overlapping chronology, plus
positive evidence in separate windows. Ordinary paired screening freezes a
matching authorized dataset receipt, while old pairs remain provisional.
The constructor records actual policy-aligned gene changes separately from
posterior retrieval. No new research data or market-positive result was
invented. See ADR-013.

## 2026-09-28 - Directional negative knowledge and diagnostic comparison

Harmful reuse now needs the exact mutation/context and independently authorized
negative windows, revalidated against original settlements; legacy losses are
not gene-wide bans. Existing durable causal jobs preregister equal compute caps
and can settle a complete-fold diagnostic comparison without compounding or
credit authority. Missing authorized data remains an explicit dependency.
See ADR-017.

## 2026-09-29 - Prospective genetic identity and bounded technical replacement

Prospective source seals now retain the exact specialist parent group separately
from canonical instrument axes. Blinded mutation shares the constructor's legal
gene mask. A semantic-parent admission failure with no evaluation run may buy
one new, linked experiment after terminal technical closure, never a scientific
retry or rewritten receipt. See the amendment to ADR-019.

## 2026-09-30 - Bounded 15k transport repair after G247

G247 built an exact three-arm triplet, but its frozen control timed out under
the generic 780s differential child limit before any scientific receipt. The
v2 probe gets a finite 1620s default/1680s ceiling; a fourth and final
source-sealed attempt is admitted only for this no-result timeout lineage.
The first three technical receipts remain immutable. See ADR-019.

## 2026-10-03 - Attested cold-start constructor repair

Fresh Academy/kernel models now seal current-family parameter identity and a
new confirmation runtime passport without overriding source parameters. One
strictly unobserved twenty-seat missing-identity quarantine may receive one
changed-source technical replacement under the original scientific scope;
original history and authority limits stay unchanged. See ADR-022.
