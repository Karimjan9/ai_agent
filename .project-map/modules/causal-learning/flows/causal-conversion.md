# Causal conversion loop

Original council conversion can preregister a bounded discovery follow-up
through its existing feedback owner. Server proof, original receipt/plan/source
and legal parameter delta are verified before the conversion kernel exposes
ready work. The sole arbiter leases it; its work consumer invokes canonical
construction, same-cohort continuation, atomic preparation and witnessed queue
admission. A stable operational hold does not mint another scientific attempt.
Support-role producer trials and original same-budget Portfolio comparisons
remain distinct from confirmed skill, qualification and economic inheritance.

Typed operator proposals have a separate bounded research admission:
`compileResearchCandidate -> decisionOperatorContract -> original closed-input
confirmation/risk-reduction/exit receipt -> exact absence/ablation comparison`.
This removes a prior-success prerequisite only for research proposals; normal
Academy admission, independent causal credit, E3/E4 and live guards remain
unchanged. Interpreter CPU or a successful benchmark task alone cannot become
economic authority. Native council feedback records event-use/maturity under
the same epoch policy rather than making paper events research data.

An operator can run `trading:audit-research-window-provenance --json` before
planning independent evidence, optionally supplying an explicit UTC candidate
interval and a JSON design with source, exact-control, intervention, context
and stopping-rule hashes. The command reads a fixed evaluation-run high water
and compact request-reference ranges, existing work status and archive metadata;
it never dumps outcome payloads, writes DB rows, dispatches or authorizes data.
References exclude overlapping physical events conservatively, but do not claim
all referenced candles were consumed. Missing training/selection/context
history blocks an unused-window conclusion. `--proposal` wraps the existing
future six-month reservation in a sealable, non-persisted draft; the caller must
retain its immutable owner preregistration before validation outcomes. Existing
paper disjointness, post-paper data and all independent evidence gates still own
execution. No historical generic instrument route is created.

An explicitly selected clean-discovery Academy cohort may observe local stage
behavior on a new calendar-selected 15000+512 four-stream scope. Its cold cap
binds physical parent/foundation/execution, not the slice/code/quote label.
The canonical arbiter still owns prepare and dispatch; no full, independent
confirmation, economic skill or inherited trait follows from this scope alone.
See ADR-024.

An explicitly attributed secondary-price discovery bundle retains the verified
original native bundle's parent price/economic hashes in a sealed budget
anchor. MTF readiness revalidates that anchor before Academy dependencies are
constructed; mixed prices and new bundle hashes cannot renew the original
physical-discovery question cap (ADR-027).

## Prospective screen-repair branch

An immutable promising pair buys one fresh, passport/phase-bound guided,
memory-blinded and unchanged-control triplet. Instrument surfaces and training
budgets are identical. Learnability separates missing data, rare context, no
effect and behavioral activation before expensive replay. Conversion closes
discovery once, without causal credit, and reserves a blocked unused-window
validation follow-up. Already searched history and 2026 paper data cannot fulfill
that dependency. See ADR-019.

The source seal includes the exact declared candidate/control semantic group.
Construction retains its specialist role and genetic envelope, while runtime
instrument context uses canonical axes; canonical `normal` cannot replace
the parent's `normal_volatility` genetic identity. The blinded selector shares
the baseline's legal mutation mask, not the guided ranking. Two distinct
constructor failures may buy bounded fresh sealed attempts after terminal
technical closure, only if none of their arms opened an evaluation run. A final
transport repair is possible only for an exact control's unobserved 780s
timeout with no decision/trade receipt. Each experiment retains the prior ID
and exact parent group. This does not reopen or relabel old generations, and
discovery still grants no causal credit.

## Trigger

The instrument path begins with a code-owned capability card, not a catalog
label. The card specifies runtime events, parameter surfaces, data/context and
required downstream stage. Ordinary assignments retain their sealed treatment
owner; only executable support surfaces present in the model are added. These
telemetry selections do not remove central safety/risk execution.

The activation frontier ranks source learnability from immutable discovery
receipts before its signal tie-breaker. It never consults live state or future
validation results. Ready exact-pair projection debt can be selected as one
arbiter action before new compute. Historical reference-only debt gets bounded
maintenance rather than monopolizing successor construction. No settled
historical market receipt is rewritten.

Generic genes such as `lookback` resolve through the sealed strategy family,
not an unrelated catalog default. Both proposed axes need source-stage
opportunity, usable data and a prospectively freezable exact control before
four screening seats are reserved. Missing instrument observations remain
unknown, not a fabricated high-uncertainty score. Zero in-scope signals leave
the source as a research hypothesis without reserving a factorial block.

For a controlled economic factorial, all C/A/B/A+B receipt identities must
agree. Main effects, interaction and leave-one-out are recomputed from four
finite observed values, recording reinforcing/interfering/additive and
incremental/redundant diagnostics. These are local research effects, not
independently confirmed instrument authority; activation discovery remains
excluded. The monitor separates reference use, causal paired results, runtime
aliases, simplified management profiles, retained descendant benefit and the
authorized-independent-data dependency (ADR-020).
Retained descendant benefit counts only an eligible same-context control and
trait-ablation comparison with an actual ablated child model identity; a
boolean claim alone is not an inherited improvement.

A terminal research observation, causal cohort request or eligible composition
settlement needs an explicit scientific classification and a next action.

## Sequence

1. Validate the versioned experiment, sealed source context/gene, exact
   control and counterfactual-arm contract before expensive or terminal work.
2. Resolve the frozen control's family and, when declared, architecture/tactic
   to one exact executable composition passport before constructing the bounded
   cohort. A missing adapter rejects planning; it never falls back across
   families.
3. For an autonomous causal confirmation, dispatch nine independently
   retryable fold jobs. Every fold evaluates guided, blinded and frozen-control
   arms together against the same sealed dataset/execution identity and writes
   one immutable fold receipt.
   The Python child bound covers three independently budgeted arms plus shared
   feature preparation (720s default, 900s ceiling); a declared 240s per-arm
   budget raises the child floor to 900s. Laravel transport and
   queued-job bounds stay above it. A timed-out fold alone is retryable and
   never contributes partial credit.
   Within one three-arm request, closed-MTF compilation is reused only when
   sealed context sources and all context-affecting parameters match; the two
   decision-only causal genes may differ without sharing an entry, signal or
   trade state.
   If screening already reached a terminal boundary without three completed
   immutable arm receipts, invalidate the counterfactual contract before fold
   dispatch; a pre-replay technical cohort cannot remain `ready_for_replay`.
4. Aggregate without replay only after all 9/9 fold receipts have unique window
   identities and complete ledgers. Project the three arms through the ordinary
   immutable full-validation boundary; a partial fold set has no learning credit.
5. Perform parity, behavior, statistical and non-target checks relevant to the
   declared claim; the replay context must match the sealed source scope.
   Existing durable three-arm jobs can also own a preregistered equal-cap
   diagnostic benchmark: all arms share fold, row and time caps and a sealed
   prospective release. Settlement requires complete hash-valid fold receipts.
   `executed_diagnostic` records the comparison but grants neither measured-CPU
   equivalence, compounding proof nor credit (ADR-017).
6. Atomically write conversion receipt, final classification and next durable
   work/deferred/terminal outcome through the canonical outbox boundary.
7. For a post-v2 three-arm confirmation, reconcile the persisted component
   escrow against the exact guided agent, frozen-control pair, settlement,
   hashes and context. Only a confirmed, proof-carrying component atomically
   writes idempotent repair and causal-skill credits. A duplicate callback
   may recover the same receipts but cannot mint a second credit. Project
   Research Mentor authority only after the credit exists; no research credit
   grants paper, performance, inheritance or parent authority.
   The transactional golden-world acceptance exercises this ledger handoff
   with synthetic facts and rolls it back; only a fresh frozen market replay
   can establish market evidence.
8. For composition results, issue component credit only for explicit,
   independently attested effects. Instrument source-component identities must
   still equal the frozen passport; a hash-valid but stale assignment is not
   an invocation or component-credit receipt.
9. For ordinary instrument research, link the replay-attested assignment to
   the exact same-generation candidate/control reservation and to the verified
   screening pair's response maps, model identities and data/execution seals.
   The constructor reservation key and the learning observation pair key have
   different purposes and need not be equal. A changed assignment hash or a
   mismatched agent/map remains diagnostic and cannot earn an instrument or
   exact-bundle posterior. Contextual activation and independent-window gates
   still apply after identity validation; a corrected link is not retroactive
   market evidence or paper authority.
   Prospective Python replay also emits an exact trade-ledger slice keyed by
   regime, volatility, session, venue phase and direction, plus the matching
   instrument activation keys. Laravel compares candidate/control only at that
   granularity. A phase-scoped legacy session-only receipt, a mixed exact/legacy
   pair or a key/context disagreement cannot write a local posterior. The
   older session slice remains diagnostic and historical evidence is not
   reclassified by this contract change. Once independently confirmed, a
   phase-local posterior may steer a successor only when that successor's
   requested context explicitly names the same venue phase; omitting the phase
   cannot turn local evidence into a session-wide preference.
   An instrument research window is admitted only when the replay manifest
   explicitly declares a post-paper research-validation source, first/last
   candles inside its authorized UTC bounds, and a dataset hash equal to the
   server-owned window manifest and candidate's frozen replay seal. Ordinary
   pre-2026 foundation screening cannot borrow this authority. The receipt is
   frozen with the pair even if its control arrives
   later; verified control equality is still mandatory before settlement.
   Posterior authority rechecks every observation's sealed window and evidence
   key, rejects overlapping intervals and reused dataset hashes, and requires
   positive observations in at least two distinct windows. An arbitrary
   `independent_window_key` or a 2026 paper candle is never confirmation.
   Component and bundle support must share the full posterior state key;
   matching only a broad requested session is not exact bundle agreement.
   If exact component and bundle posteriors guide a later mutation, persist a
   separate successor receipt only when the model's actual parameter diff
   includes that recommended gene in the same family, phase and context.
   A retrieval-only policy records `not_applied`, not inheritance or paper
   authority.
10. For cold-start activation factorial, compare four immutable screening runs
    on the same execution, MTF, data, session and paired candle universe.
    Python emits one shared `paired_context_id` per candle and a compact
    no-signal opportunity-universe digest alongside sparse decision receipts.
    Laravel verifies those against each arm's immutable response and frozen
    C/A/B/AB identity. Fewer than the preregistered paired or signal
    opportunities means `underpowered_activation`, not a negative claim.
    A context unique to AB may yield only a behavioral activation hypothesis.
    The future validation interval, six monthly windows, exact comparator and
    power thresholds are preregistered with the discovery proposal and bound
    into the four-arm manifest. The conversion kernel hashes that same plan
    into one idempotent semantic receipt and a blocked next-work item. The
    reserved interval starts after the 2026 paper-only epoch; no replay or
    credit is allowed until a separate research epoch and dataset are
    authorized and sealed. Economic effect, component credit, Mentor and
   promotion remain unearned.
    Before prospective construction, seal the intended confirmation route:
    historical causal research uses its existing frozen causal contract;
    Academy/activation/instrument independent authority requires original
    preregistered unused authorized-window provenance. Missing provenance is
    `BLOCKED_DEPENDENCY`, never a renamed discovery window or fake credit.
    Activation reservations also reject overlap with any approved future paper
    period; the 2026 paper-only cutoff never moves.

## Rules

- Academy uses the arbiter's `OPEN_ACADEMY_EXPERIMENT` to atomically prepare
  one draft and pollable trial intent, then `DISPATCH_ACADEMY_EXPERIMENT` to
  admit that same latest generation via the canonical dispatcher. Research-only
  immutable screening can settle stage proof but cannot grant paper/credit.
- With no ready prospective trial, the same owner may preview a bounded
  hypothesis-only cold start from archived executable parameters. Its durable
  decision precedes new passport/trial creation and current pre-2026 data/MTF/
  execution sealing. The preview and actual compiler hashes must agree. One
  cold-start cohort consumes the stable data/execution scope budget; a source
  edit is not another discovery allowance. Old source proof is never reused.
  One attested pre-execution missing-identity quarantine may buy one linked
  technical replacement under changed source, but only with all twenty
  quarantines and no evaluation, artifact, gate outcome or score. The original
  question/data/scope/cap and immutable history remain unchanged.
  A distinct preparation-only 900-second timeout may receive one repair only
  after the original sealed request/checkpoint and all technical envelopes
  prove zero scientific output. The approved disposition precedes scoped batch
  cancellation and active/queue drain; original receipts are not rewritten.
  The same arbiter prepares fresh source/IDs with unchanged primary and sixteen
  preregistered kernel vectors. Original + constructor + preparation is capped
  at three cohorts, and no scientific budget or independent evidence is reset.
  A distinct original clean-discovery native MTF validator refusal instead
  admits at most one `academy_unobserved_mtf_validator_replacement_v1` under
  fresh full/Python seals. All attempted controls and never-executed exact
  dependents are re-attested before the laboratory lock consumes the allowance.
  All twenty original vectors/data/cost stay exact; original plus one is capped
  at two and excludes any constructor/preparation replacement chain (ADR-025).
- Before persisting Academy or kernel models, constructors stamp current-family
  schema-canonical parameter and local-adapter identities. Admission does not
  repair those hashes or waive real parameter changes. Confirmation research
  receives a new explicit-owner `confirmation_entry_mtf` passport and exact,
  no-override parameter-preserving management; an old unrelated passport is
  provenance only. The MTF protocol and all four source seals remain mandatory.
- Creation stamps canonical primary frozen-control ownership plus admission-only
  exact control IDs after hypothesis cleanup; kernel pair metadata carries its
  own actual control/candidate IDs. Candidate admission waits for that control's
  immutable replay and projection, never another same-family reference.
  The Academy primary instrument owner revalidates the complete compiled roster,
  exact baseline/deltas and common context/source before selecting one shared
  surface. This separate multi-arm reservation is not an ordinary economic
  pair and cannot create instrument authority from stage proof.
- Before any fresh-discovery writer, the arbiter checks ready earned director
  work and revalidates an Academy curriculum successor against its original
  settled trial and exact immutable source. Mere stage depth/planned status is
  not earned work. Existing closure/recovery/durable obligations keep precedence;
  one stable-input cold cohort may precede new discovery without preempting them.
  The read-only director result is reused, and unchanged decisions remain deduped.
- Stage comparisons require `replay_decision_identity_v2`: one chronological
  candle domain, attested consumed context streams and as-of joins, and exact
  upstream semantic hashes outside the declared causal footprint. Direction,
  geometry and entry-linked trade exits/costs/outcomes affect stage identity;
  aggregate count equality is diagnostic only. Setup topology owns
  context/location/setup. Missing identities are unassessable, not no-effect.
- Academy source admission precedes local proof and trial planning. Only the
  original complete immutable run with exact agent/model/parameter and observed
  data/execution identity can freeze a prospective source. The Oracle adapter
  preserves malformed-contract reasons and unknown entry/realized values.
  Cold curriculum checks the actual runtime model's parameter consumption.
  Complete primary-arm evidence yields per-arm stage comparisons; eligible
  positive controllability nominates a distinct candidate-baseline passport,
  never a retrospective baseline update or economic skill credit.
  A prospective source/roster guard failure remains terminal unassessable:
  reconciliation counts its closure and conversion creates an evidence-repair
  dependency, not positive-candidate replication, regardless of raw deltas.
- Instrument validation statistics are partitioned by authorized research
  epoch and exact gene/old/new/baseline/evaluator/context identity. Discovery
  observations do not enter those statistics or acquire retrospective authority.
  Each proof rechecks the original source pair/runs and server-authorized window.
- Idea submission freezes its full experimental design. Allocation either
  matches actual bounded intervention, context, species, block and supported
  stopping rule, or records a deferred-design reason without claiming it.

- Directional learning v2 groups the exact gene, old/new values, direction,
  context and source identity. Harmful reuse requires three authorized,
  non-overlapping negative windows with no positive window and revalidation
  of the original verified settlements. It vetoes only that exact delta in
  the exact context; legacy lessons remain hypotheses, not gene-wide bans.
- Readiness reports `awaiting_authorized_research_data` when no completed
  authorized independent window exists. Future dates are not available data;
  2026 paper-only observations cannot be substituted.
- Control identity is frozen; a genetic parent is not a causal control by name alone.
- Lesson, source pair, frozen control, baseline model and composition passport
  must describe the same executable family. Declared architecture and tactic
  must also match exactly.
- A stale causal construction dependency may preserve population cardinality
  only as an explicit non-credit diagnostic control with the causal experiment
  and promotion authority removed.
- At-least-once delivery requires idempotent consumer behavior; hashes alone do
  not create exactly-once distributed execution.
- The outcome must preserve uncertainty and reason codes rather than forcing a
  causal or economic conclusion.
- A later economic receipt may add evidence, but cannot erase an already sealed
  mentor-window proof. Explicit drift/revocation remains a separate transition.
- Research Mentor authority is local and research-only. Economic Parent
  authority additionally requires the sealed 2026 paper rung and the existing
  descendant/context-trust requirements.
- Fold retries are scoped to one fold. Exhausted retries quarantine the causal
  experiment technically and cannot turn completed sibling folds into credit.
- Candidate contracts are keyed by immutable LabAgent identity; a shared
  strategy label cannot exchange guided, blinded or frozen-control ownership.
- Candidate screening admission resolves the exact control agent from its
  sealed pair contract (or persisted experiment identity), not from any other
  same-family control in the generation. Missing or conflicting control IDs
  fail closed before replay.
- A terminal incomplete screening triplet grants no causal credit and is closed
  as `invalid_counterfactual_contract`; the arbiter must then reselect instead
  of repeatedly dispatching a no-op full-validation command.
- A guided, blinded or paired candidate stopped before its own replay by
  `FROZEN_CONTROL_REPLAY_INCOMPLETE` inherits the exact same-generation
  control's technical disposition. A terminal control creates terminal
  diagnostic history, not a fabricated independent transient-recovery debt;
  causal triplet ownership is resolved from the persisted experiment when the
  candidate metadata has no direct control id.
- In the post-v2 epoch, lattice risk/context failure leaves the experiment
  provisional even if its paired window comparisons passed. A synthetic
  status flag, a selected legacy lesson or an unlinked escrow cannot create
  repair or causal-skill ledger credit.
- Prospective repair discovery seals exactly 15,000 evaluated M5 candles plus
  512 separate warmup candles, the UTC bounds, month counts, dataset and
  execution identities. Python must replay that evaluated slice, return the
  same contract hash and complete receipt, and Laravel must reject a missing
  or shortened receipt before learnability settlement. Three arms require the
  same sealed window; this discovery is never independent validation.
- Prospective exact-context quote quality is measured on that evaluated slice
  after calendar/phase/regime/volatility/transition matching but before the
  liquidity veto. Global archive quote coverage cannot stand in for this
  context. The immutable receipt binds scope hash, actual rows/UTC bounds,
  observed counts and synchronized BID/ASK provenance; missing or insufficient
  context quotes are `data_missing`, while a valid zero-opportunity context is
  `underpowered`. Modeled costs are not observed quotes, and no gate is widened.
- A prospective repair cohort is the three-arm atomic unit, not a 20-seat
  discovery portfolio. It uses the canonical generation/dispatch lifecycle,
  but unrelated novelty-seat failures cannot quarantine its exact comparison.
- Future prospective repair sources seal the source generation's MTF bundle
  hash into the source identity and preregistered validation plan. The source
  is ineligible if that hash is missing or disagrees with either exact pair
  data hash; a historical plan with an empty MTF identity is not silently
  upgraded or treated as independent validation evidence.
- The 15k M5 probe has a distinct, finite Python child budget (default 1620s,
  ceiling 1680s) beneath Laravel's 1800s batch transport and 2400s queue
  lease. The generic differential screen remains bounded at 780-840s. G247's
  control reached only an opportunity checkpoint before that generic timeout:
  it is technical history, not a negative strategy or an observed trial. One
  v2-sealed transport repair is possible only after the two earlier constructor
  repairs and only while no arm has a scientific receipt; a fourth failed
  attempt exhausts the source.

## Terminal publication and measured learning

Replay completion is published only after request/response, trade ledger and
decision trace artifacts have been sealed. Post-commit learning delivery cannot
observe a completed run with unfinished artifacts. New trace manifests retain
the producer's requested/complete/count proof and actual decision coverage;
legacy artifacts are not backfilled as new producer-attested evidence.

The ordinary causal planner preregisters `causal_selector_observation_v1` before
fold execution: exact baseline/legal space, source-memory exposure, independent
blinded choice and scoped selection wall time. Blind selection does not exclude
the guided treatment; equal independently selected treatments are a null
comparison. `TypedInstrumentFoundryService` binds the three actual arm vectors
and immutable folds to that receipt. Selection wall time and economic replay
CPU remain separate; raw positive outcomes are not memory superiority.

Progress separates technical completion, answer-producing comparisons and
actual descendant/trait-ablation witnesses. Historical Academy/causal research
is not the authorized post-paper instrument route. Missing independent data
remains a dependency, not a provisional result silently promoted to authority.

## Research-question improvement

The existing portfolio may rank compatible ready questions using prospective
forecast/information/cost evidence and a seeded random-control share. Academy
tasks seal learning-progress/prerequisite identity only when newly inserted.
Native causal folds preregister a memory forecast before dispatch and calibrate
only after original atomic settlement. Committed screening projection records
bounded original behavioral descriptors. Foundry mines typed CALL abstractions
only from distinct solved tasks, preserving the expanded primitive program.

Cheap rejection audits and masked/unmasked measurement studies preregister
their own exact questions before later witnesses. The existing arbiter remains
the sole dispatcher. Fixed policy challengers may be compared on six synthetic
worlds and prospectively sealed unobserved real questions; neither path skips
authorized data, scientific gates or separate measured search/transfer proof.
See ADR-028 and `docs/operations/research-meta-learning.md`.
