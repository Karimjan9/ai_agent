# Causal learning states

`trading:audit-research-window-provenance` produces a bounded read-only
`BLOCKED_DEPENDENCY` receipt from research request chronology, archive inventory
and existing work owners. Missing original training/selection/context exposure
is explicit uncertainty; absent JSON keys and renamed provider/hash/timeframe
labels never demonstrate unused physical market events. Its optional future
design produces `draft_preregistration_not_persisted` through the existing
ActivationValidationPlan owner, binding exact-control/intervention/context and
stopping-rule hashes, UTC interval and exclusion policy. This draft has no
dataset SHA or server authorization and cannot execute or grant authority.
The immutable owner preregistration must precede validation outcomes; actual
authorized data and original exposure proof remain required.

Clean-discovery cold cohorts seal the stored bundle and `pre_2026_discovery_only`
identity before admission. Their allowance binds foundation, physical parent
fork/economic rows and execution; new slice, quote coverage, code or label cannot
replenish it. Local stage proof is not independent/economic authority. Full
validation and the authorized independent-window dependency remain separate
(ADR-024).

An original terminal/drained clean-discovery validator refusal may propose one
`academy_unobserved_mtf_validator_replacement_v1`. Attempted controls require
their exact immutable native error/request/runtime/release evidence;
unexecuted dependents require the canonical exact-control refusal. Any observed
science, missing arm or other failure shape denies it. The arbiter prepares
fresh IDs and full/Python seals with all twenty original vectors/data/cost
unchanged. Original plus one is terminally capped; no other replacement can
follow that leaf. Old evidence and independent/paper authority remain unchanged
(ADR-025).
That same original-evidence proof owns the classifier's terminal diagnostic
projection; absence of outputs alone or an error string is insufficient.
The projection never reopens the old trial or changes its technical receipt.

Academy trial `planned -> materialized` creates one locked draft plus pending
canonical-admission intent. `pending -> admitted` requires the arbiter-selected
canonical dispatcher and successful snapshot admission for that same generation.
Restart reuses the trial's existing generation. A complete, hash-valid arm set
may settle stage proof from immutable screening; it is still research-only.
Missing event identity is non-controlling, never count-based mastery.
It is explicitly `unassessable`, not scientific `no_effect`, and cannot retire
an axis. A complete `replay_decision_identity_v2` comparison can demonstrate
changed semantics even with identical candle sets/counts. Repeated delivery of
one immutable comparison cannot increase its retirement observation count.

An Academy source requires a completed exact-agent/model/parameter immutable
run and the original matching diagnostic/data/execution projection. Invalid or
historical projections remain indexed, not executable source authority. Cold
curriculum plans only axes consumed by the effective runtime entry model;
inactive location adapters and declarative-only designs fail closed. Tested
stage, observed depth and controllable local proof are distinct. Eligible
candidate advancement creates a new baseline/source-scoped successor passport;
it does not overwrite the old baseline or assert independent economic mastery.

When no prospective trial exists, `prospective_academy_cold_start_v1` can
propose archived executable parameters as a hypothesis only. A read-only
preview does not create a passport, trial or mastery. A durable arbiter
`OPEN_ACADEMY_EXPERIMENT` decision seals current pre-2026 foundation, MTF,
execution and evaluator identities before creating a new depth-zero passport
and exact arm set. A separate `DISPATCH_ACADEMY_EXPERIMENT` publishes that same
generation. Cold-start budget is stable for the sealed research-data/execution
scope; changing code alone does not renew it. Old Oracle observations and
exhausted Edge compiler budgets are not upgraded or reset.
The exception is one `academy_unobserved_identity_replacement_v1` technical
attempt: the original must be terminal with exactly twenty attested draft
missing-identity quarantines, no evaluation runs/artifacts/gate outcomes/scores,
and a changed source. The replacement retains the original question, raw data,
execution and stable scientific cap; it creates new IDs, not a reopened trial.
Any observed result, other failure shape or already-used replacement blocks it.

A separately approved `academy_preparation_containment_v1` moves an exact
twenty-seat preparation-only timeout cohort through `draining -> terminal`.
The original 900-second control error, sealed request and request-bound
`snapshot_loaded` checkpoint must agree, with no scientific result/gate/trade.
The durable disposition is written before cancellation of only its stored
generation-owned batches; active work is never killed. Canceled stale jobs
consume normally, and untouched agents close only after active/queue drain.
One `academy_preparation_containment_repair_v1` may then prepare fresh IDs
under changed full/Python source, preserving the primary compiler and all
sixteen preregistered kernel vectors. Original + constructor + preparation
attempts is at most three; neither source edits nor labels replenish it.
An original native unexpected-candle-gap failure remains immutable technical
history and becomes a data-quality dependency, not another preparation allowance.
The same M5 bytes cannot open a fourth attempt. Verified prospective provider
repair may establish a different actual-data scope through ordinary canonical
freeze; cold readiness waits for its own selected-dataset quoted bundle and does
not re-use the failed generation's quotes or claim independent validation.
The shared recovery classifier revalidates the original artifact/approval
chain before calling this terminal diagnostic, not a strategy result or credit.

Fresh primary/kernel models receive current schema-canonical fingerprints and
local-adapter identities at construction. JSON order/integral-number hydration
is representation, but a real post-seal value change still fails admission.
The Academy-owned confirmation adapter freezes a new matching passport and
parameter-preserving management before creation; exact raw risk/parameter values
remain unchanged. Generic planner rotation cannot select this owner-only spec.

Selection is continuation-first: existing closure, recovery and durable work
retain ownership. A ready curriculum successor must revalidate its original
settled trial, exact arm roster, immutable source and candidate-baseline proof;
stage depth or `would_materialize` alone cannot earn precedence. Attested
Academy and director continuations precede every fresh repair, market-drift,
degradation and generic discovery branch, even after the cold cap is spent.
One bounded ready cold-start cohort precedes new discovery only. A single
read-only director plan is reused; this creates neither a second scheduler nor
new authority. Direct trial settlement checks the same frozen evaluator,
execution, bundle and actual four-stream seals as normal materializer settlement.
New prospective trials with missing/invalid source or primary-arm evidence close as
`settled_unassessable_stage_evidence`, not a scientific no-effect result or an
axis-retirement observation. Full-runtime and Python-loaded fingerprints have
separate frozen fields; neither can substitute for the other.
This terminal is counted by reconciliation and carries an unassessable
behavioral summary plus a blocked technical evidence-repair dependency, never
a positive-candidate economic replication from descriptive power or deltas.
Legacy complete marginal observations may retain descriptive power/density
classification, but their missing stage proof remains unassessable and grants
no advancement, causal authority or retirement count.

Instrument `validation_epochs` v2 are separate proof scopes. Discovery/legacy
aggregates remain research observations; confirmation is re-derived only from
one exact-delta authorized epoch. A different delta's provisional evidence does
not erase a valid epoch, and an authorized receipt never retroactively upgrades
old discovery rows. Successor consumption must match the original old/new delta.

`prospective_screen_repair` construction uses the ordinary three-arm enrollment
and exact-parity guards. Terminal complete screening closes discovery as
`data_missing`, `underpowered`, `no_effect`, or `ready_for_independent_validation`.
Missing data/power creates blocked new-opportunity work, not economic validation
or harmful skill. Ready discovery creates blocked independent-validation work.
Ordinary outcome callbacks and the credit bridge reject this discovery kind;
later authority needs a separate admitted canonical validation contract.
An exact-control run that ends solely in the 780s generic differential timeout
has technical evidence but no scientific result. After two distinct constructor
repairs, it may open one new v2-sealed transport attempt with a finite 15k
budget. A completed decision/trade receipt or a fourth failed attempt closes
that source for retry; old attempts remain immutable.
The experiment remains provisional with no credit. No-effect closes its bounded
probe; other states create one blocked authorized-window dependency. Missing data
and insufficient power project to EPISODIC knowledge, never harmful skill.

The module intentionally does **not** use one overloaded `status` field as its
entire model. Before changing a transition, identify its dimension.

| Dimension | Representative states | Owner / meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work lifecycle | planned → ready → leased → running → settling → settled | Work/outbox owner; lease, retry and idempotency behavior. |
| Evidence | valid, incomplete, invalid, selection_contaminated | Evidence/receipt owner; whether a result may be interpreted. |
| Behavioral claim | untested, unreachable, no_effect_on_probe, stage_controllable | Conversion policy; what the intervention established. |
| Economic claim | underpowered, harmful, inconclusive, positive_candidate, independently_replicated | Statistical/settlement policy; not a promotion shortcut. |
| Reuse authority | research_only, confirmed_component, mentor, descendant_proven, eligible_parent | Authority/skill owner; earned separately from claims. |
| Deployment eligibility | blocked, paper_observing, eligible | Paper/admission owner; never inferred from a single score. |
| Freshness | active, drift_suspected, hibernating, revoked | Runtime/evidence policy. |

Durable causal fold work has its own lifecycle:
`planned -> running -> completed`, with failures moving to `retry_ready` and,
after bounded retries, `technical_error`. `CausalFoldExecutionService` owns the
lease and preserves completed sibling folds.
An eventual `completed` receipt keeps its attempt count. A prior timeout can
therefore be recovered for scientific settlement without becoming a clean
unattended autonomy proof.
Once the experiment is `technical_quarantine` or
`invalid_counterfactual_contract`, any still-queued sibling fold exits before
creating or executing replay evidence.

## Core transition rules

Instrument capability is distinct from invocation/authority. A code-owned card
is `executable_parameter_adapter`, `structural_proxy_adapter`, or
`declarative_only`. Source learnability is `unknown_data`, `data_missing`,
`underpowered`, `no_effect` (no bound instrument parameter surface), or
`learnable`; it ranks discovery compute only and grants no economic credit.
Management profile adapters explicitly expose their simplified single-partial
engine and unsupported abstract plan features.
An activation proposal is `not_proposed` when either intervention axis lacks
observed source-stage opportunity, data, or a prospective exact-control path.
Unmeasured evaluation count stays unknown rather than becoming an exploration
score. Generic parameter names inherit the sealed strategy-family owner;
source records are never retroactively relabelled to make them eligible.

An open attested instrument row with a verified exact control may be reconciled
through the arbiter-owned bounded SQL command. Candidate rows keep the ordinary
paired-context posterior guards. Frozen-control rows transition from
`awaiting_paired_control` to `control_reference_consumed`, with no contribution
or posterior credit, only after assignment, reservation, run and context hashes
agree. Row locks make reference closure idempotent. Already terminal rows stay
unchanged. Unverified/stale reservations remain unconsumed, not fabricated proof.

Directional learning v2 may move a harmful hypothesis to confirmed only with
three distinct authorized non-overlapping negative windows and zero positive
windows under the same exact mutation and context. Retrieval revalidates the
original settlements and constrains that delta, not the whole gene. A legacy
confirmed label alone grants no such authority. The equal-cap benchmark moves
`planned -> executed_diagnostic` only after the complete attested fold set;
neither state implies confirmed compounding or credit (ADR-017).

1. A planned experiment becomes runnable only with a sealed, valid contract and
   a compatible data/execution context.
   A prospective repair source is ineligible when its generation's frozen MTF
   bundle hash is absent or differs from either exact pair data hash. Its
   preregistered future validation plan carries that source hash; an older
   plan with an empty MTF identity is not backfilled into new evidence.
   A prospective repair's genetic parent group is distinct from canonical
   instrument context. A pre-replay semantic-parent failure may be followed
   by one separate constructor-incomplete failure; each failed attempt remains
   technical-terminal, all three arms must be quarantined, and no arm may have
   an evaluation run. Only a changed source/evaluator seal permits the final
   replacement. Three attempts exhaust the budget; any observed replay blocks
   replacement. Prior attempts retain no strategy or credit authority.
2. A worker may settle only the current leased attempt; a stale worker cannot
   overwrite newer evidence.
3. Terminal evidence creates an immutable conversion receipt plus exactly one
   explicit next durable action, deferred reason or terminal reason.
4. `INCONCLUSIVE`, `UNDERPOWERED`, `UNREACHABLE` and technical quarantine are
   valid terminal results; none may be re-labelled as positive authority.
5. Component credit needs a declared paired/attested effect rather than a
   whole-packet performance result.
   A venue-phase-scoped instrument additionally needs a powered candidate and
   control trade slice in the same exact phase and an activation receipt for
   that exact key. Session-only history cannot cross this posterior boundary.
   Even an exact paired slice remains provisional until every observation has
   a server-authorized, immutable post-paper research-window receipt. Three
   unique names without distinct non-overlapping datasets, or two positive
   observations confined to one window, cannot become confirmed authority.
6. `confirmed_component` requires the observed replay context to match the
   sealed source context; a cross-session/context result remains research-only.
7. A confirmed mentor proof is monotonic under later forward/economic receipts.
   It may be hibernated or revoked by the explicit freshness policy, but must
   not disappear merely because a later receipt omits repeated window counts.
8. Historical forward evidence can prepare a paper candidate, but Economic
   Parent/performance authority requires valid prospective evidence from the
   frozen 2026 paper epoch or an explicitly authorized, freeze-bound future
   paper period disjoint from original research validation (ADR-023).
9. A durable causal experiment can settle only with the exact configured fold
   index set, complete request/response/data/execution hashes and a completed
   aggregate receipt. Partial folds are diagnostic only.
10. A pre-replay experiment whose three arms are terminal without three
    completed immutable screening runs moves from `ready_for_replay` to
    `invalid_counterfactual_contract`. It cannot stay open, dispatch folds or
    receive scientific/performance/inheritance credit.
11. A candidate quarantined only because its same-generation frozen control
    terminated inherits that control's disposition. It owns no separate replay
    recovery debt unless the referenced control is itself transient-recoverable.
12. A post-v2 causal experiment crosses `provisional -> confirmed` only when
    the component lattice is proof-carrying and every risk/context check passes.
    The confirmed triplet then writes one atomic repair-credit and causal-skill-
    credit pair keyed to its exact escrow; retries reuse those event identities.
    Mentor projection follows the credit receipt and remains research-only.
13. `activation_factorial` work can end as `underpowered_activation`,
    `local_activation_not_observed`, `activation_observed_nonexclusive`, or a
    joint tactic/entry activation hypothesis. These are behavioral states,
    not economic claims. A positive behavioral hypothesis creates a semantic
    receipt and blocked fresh-window validation item. The future interval and
    proof criteria were hashed before discovery, but the 2026 paper epoch is
    excluded and no research dataset is authorized yet; a reserved interval
    is not an executable data receipt or causal credit. It never reaches the
    credit bridge. Incomplete/mismatched arm evidence remains invalid and
   cannot be reinterpreted as a scientific no-effect result.
    New activation plans also seal `sealed_confirmation_route_v1` and an
    explicit `BLOCKED_DEPENDENCY` reason; a future paper-period overlap is a
    separate data-policy blocker. Historical causal research is a distinct
    existing frozen-contract route and does not wait for live 2026 candles.
    `activation_future_research_window_v2` adds that route. Historical v1
    plans are validated against their original non-executable reservation
    contract and are not backfilled or upgraded into executable validation.
