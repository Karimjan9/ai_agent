# ADR-002: Contextual proof ratchets before a sealed paper epoch

## Decision

Research evidence is accumulated as context-bound, proof-carrying component and
bundle claims. A later receipt cannot silently erase an already sealed mentor
proof, and a local claim cannot become global authority. Historical research is
restricted to data before 2026; a frozen candidate may use 2026 only as
prospective paper/forward evidence.

## Why

All-or-nothing promotion discarded useful causal micro-wins, while assignment-
only instrument attribution and context leakage could manufacture false
progress. Conversely, letting historical forward results create an Economic
Parent would contaminate the reserved paper year and make the final rung
self-referential.

## Consequences

- A Research Mentor remains local, research-only and reusable only inside its
  sealed context/gene scope.
- Actual runtime activation, exact control and independent evidence are required
  for component or bundle credit.
- Confirmed mentor evidence is monotonic unless an explicit freshness/drift
  transition hibernates or revokes it.
- An E3 candidate is frozen before observation; only valid 2026 prospective
  evidence can create E4 performance/economic authority.
- Paper observations cannot alter mutation, screening, selection or instrument
  posteriors, and a changed candidate needs a new untouched future epoch.
