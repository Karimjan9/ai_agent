# ADR-037: All data from 2026 onward remains paper-only

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: Accepted

The user reserves every observation from 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z onward for paper,
including 2027 and every later year. A finite annual paper observation window
does not create a research opening at its end. This supersedes the future
research-period assumptions in earlier epoch and scoped-proof designs.

Laravel's epoch owner, historical archive intake, dataset exports, research
window admission, scoped component/selector/descendant registration and Python
source/transport guards enforce the same fixed boundary. Configuration, year
labels and even server signatures cannot authorize research from paper data.
The restriction includes selection, mutation, warmup and all context streams.

New activation plans declare missing genuinely unexposed authorized pre-2026
original windows; they have no invented future interval and cannot execute.
Existing immutable reservations, runs and paper seals are not rewritten or
upgraded into proof. Annual future paper windows retain their frozen candidate,
observation-clock and ordinary risk/evidence requirements.

Original preregistration, complete training/selection exposure provenance,
independent window power, exact controls and descendant disjointness remain
mandatory. A software test or a discovery replay cannot satisfy market proof.
Without genuine original untouched historical evidence, validation, mentor,
inheritance and economic authority remain explicit data dependencies.
