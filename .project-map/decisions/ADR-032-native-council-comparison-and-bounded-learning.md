# ADR-032 - Native comparisons, marked paper risk and bounded research consumption

## Context

An ordinary legacy SOLO replay does not necessarily share the native council's
actual decision clock or account metrics. Paper reservation also cannot ignore
open losses or reconstruct an old equity peak from a missing cost ledger.
Archive inventory and interpreter telemetry alone do not show that learning
changed research selection or reduced search effort.

## Decision

Future plans may explicitly preregister `specialist_council_native_solo_v1`.
The stored original council determines the single member and unchanged
allocation, program, horizon, passport and risk. Original native account
receipts and physical clocks still own comparison. This is a matched-member
allocation comparison, not a best standalone full-budget claim. Neither a new
signature nor follow-up remapping upgrades an old plan without the declaration.

An original `specialist_council_native_chosen_solo_v1` may instead explicitly
preregister one `chosen_source_unqualified` at full-account allocation. The
server seals the exact original compiled member and changes only allocation
to 1.0; programme/passport/horizon/account/cost/external-risk identity stays
equal. Original unused sources and arms are mandatory. This is not a ranked
best-qualified SOLO claim and cannot be added through observed continuation.

Paper intake locks the account, marks owned positions from fresh closed candles
and charges their pinned exit/carry costs. New risk is bounded by the lesser of
realized balance and marked equity. Only actually observed peaks are recorded.
The additive nullable peak column has no historical backfill; missing marks or
unknown historical peak blocks new intake, not management of owned positions.

Behavior archives feed only a capped, original-evidence-verified research prior
with a consumption receipt. Original withheld projections remain withheld.
Finite library/blinded search uses one prospectively sealed registered semantic
pool, typed task, verified macro sources and equal attempt/node/CPU caps. Actual
native products measure local effort; task familiarity and incomplete products
cannot become general learning or economic authority.

A separate prospectively sealed native spread-context study changes only the
computed instrument `spread_liquidity_state`. Raw observations, program,
execution cost and hard-risk definitions are unchanged. Actual gate and final
Entry/WAIT events are recorded in-loop. Paired sensitivity requires common raw
opportunities, quote/as-of provenance, reached gates and matching pre-gate
account state. Unreached or diverged events are explicit excluded coverage.
The original immutable paired consumer is idempotent and research-only.

The two terminal traces retain the existing retryable candle-event audit
projection. Ordinary historical-learning/advice and causal source readers
exclude the study's reserved declarations and original runs; old mixed cache
identities are not reused. Audit visibility must not turn masked observations
into generic failure-directed mutation advice or a second outcome.

Native attestation verifies the actual received trace against its producer hash
and sealed native identity before replay-use recording. HTTP transport preserves
integral float types; the existing generic canonical hash is not replaced.
Matching copied hashes do not authenticate changed actual data. Zero primary
Entry/WAIT effect under unchanged risk/cost is a valid research-only result.

Its original six-slot intent uses a distinct `spread_context_study` purpose:
four pristine sources and two originally declared masked/unmasked carriers.
The exact target/context and supported spread predicate are intent-owned.
An explicit fixed-vocabulary `liquidity_atr_binding` selects the same real
closed source field for the observer and instrument gate; its key/value hash
is receipted. Missing/invalid values are not filled from another field or a
synthetic constant. Legacy unstated context behavior is unchanged.
Atomic preparation installs the target predicate through the existing membership
cell before all six canonical assignments and hashes. Both carriers keep the
same native program. Only those two dispatch; original source references and
six constructor episodes settle neutrally after valid whole-pair evidence.
The existing terminal boundary reconciles immutable technical pairs too, without
inventing a sensitivity result or a source replay. Active/missing arms remain
dependencies; old roots and comparison plans cannot acquire this purpose.

One named ordinary unadmitted draft may pass an explicit read-only
`unsealed_draft_cold_maintenance_v1` check after drain-first STOP. Actual complete
construction, absent constructor ownership, known-empty queues and absence of
all source/admission/evaluation seals and attempts distinguish it from frozen
work. Protected native follow-up/panel/Academy owners remain excluded. Default
rolling preflight stays strict; authenticated physical idle and actual new boot
proof remain required. Deployment neither retires nor reseals that draft, and
its original missing-data admission prerequisite remains unchanged.

A verified full-source M5 gap dependency on a complete ordinary unadmitted
historical draft is commandless WAIT, not repeated settlement. Its physical
source/proof/calendar watermark is durable selection evidence. Unknown queues,
incomplete construction, protected purposes and invalid/drifted source proof
remain outside this exception; genuine unchanged settlement retains its cap.

## Limits

These protocols do not create a generation, alternative dispatcher or scheduler.
Canonical construction, readiness, locks, source/data seals and immutable arm
publication remain mandatory. Deployment is not execution or qualification.
Old original artifacts and the exhausted one-completion root are not rewritten,
replayed or replenished. 2026 remains paper-only. Missing untouched authorized
windows, broker netting, tick/latency fills and broker rollover evidence remain
explicit dependencies; no fixture or observed decision change supplies them.
