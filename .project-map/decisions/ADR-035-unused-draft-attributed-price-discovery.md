# ADR-035: Explicit unused-draft attributed-price discovery

Status: accepted for explicitly approved prospective research; production
registration remains subject to current source/boot and unused-draft guards.

## Context

The native pre-2026 archive has seventeen unresolved M5 price intervals. A
separately verified mixed archive has real attributed replacement prices, but
cannot prove native price/volume/quote equivalence or untouched validation. The
existing G263 draft contains twenty unused genomes and ten frozen cooperative
pairs. Reconstructing it, weakening native full readiness, reusing an exhausted
Root1 allowance or interpreting its old pair owner economically would change
the question or authority.

## Decision

Add a narrow explicit preparation and receipt owner for this exact unused draft.
Persisted operator approval registers a new bounded prospective attributed-price
question; it neither prepares nor dispatches. The ordinary arbiter alone selects
preparation and canonical singleton dispatch under its current decision and
control revision. Freeze original constructor, twenty model/vector and allocation
hashes, ten control-pair hashes, native dependency and verified mixed MTF byte/
attribution/source identities. Physical-question identity cannot renew under
labels, source changes, dataset labels or repeated cadence.

Retain 15000 evaluated rows plus 512 feature warmup and the existing ordinary
200 execution warmup: true observed decision coverage is 14800 unique candles.
ENTRY/WAIT/EXIT counts are event counts, not necessarily one decision per candle.
Only actual stored compressed original artifacts can support measured receipts.
One attempt per model is maximum. Genuine terminal technical originals retain
their status/artifacts and receive unassessable operational receipts; dependent
unexecuted candidates receive genuine exact-control refusals, not fabricated
replays or measured zero effects.

Close twenty original mutation-selection episodes and ten original pairs in an
own neutral namespace after known terminal dispositions and owned queue drain.
All reward, credit and authority remain zero. Ordinary economic/causal settlement,
mutation advice, full validation, candidate gate, outbox and paper promotion are
denied regardless of diagnostic gate results. Native full readiness and all
ordinary model/gate/transport criteria remain unchanged. Existing callbacks,
dispatcher and terminal owner are reused; no scheduler, manual generation or
production SQL path is added.

## Consequences

Verified real prices can answer a bounded model-behavior question without falsely
repairing native gaps or certifying economics. Missing native quotes/volume and
independent/future validation remain external prerequisites. Intent/preparation
failure leaves immutable identities and commandless WAIT, not renewed budget.
Only undelivered command publication has at most two transport retries; no
executed or semantically deferred scientific attempt is retried.
Terminal originals are not ordinary actionable recovery debt. Unrelated earned
work retains priority. The terminal commandless WAIT binds fresh canonical native
readiness and actual physical M5/calendar digest, not only the old stored gap
copy. Genuine future native repair changes readiness/state without authorizing
replay, full validation or credit for the spent mixed-price question.
