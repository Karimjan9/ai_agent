# ADR-007: A consumed organism requires one completed decision witness

## Decision

The frozen composition compiles to the explicit v2 typed decision graph. Its
program protocol is sealed in the runtime contract; a v1 passport cannot be
silently reinterpreted. Each strategy opportunity keeps a decision ID bound to
its source candle, aggregate manifest and program hash through typed entry,
instrument scope, MTF permission, risk, fill and managed trade close. Every
signal receipt and first veto is retained; only the inspection sample is
bounded. `consumed` requires one accepted, ordered and closed decision witness
in addition to valid bindings and node receipts. Laravel settlement requires
that witness explicitly.

The typed gate emits its pre-entry stage chain when it admits or vetoes the
signal; final trace assembly reads that hash-bound chain instead of recomputing
predicates after replay. Instrument assignments are derived from all four
frozen passport source components. A cached assignment that is hash-valid but
belongs to stale component identities is not reusable.

## Why

All component bindings can be valid while the tactic vetoes every signal.
Aggregate node counters can also be assembled from different decisions and
mistaken for one functioning organism. A management evaluation at entry is not
proof that the position lifecycle completed.

## Consequences

- A port, topology, program-version, authority or semantic-scope mismatch is a
  pre-replay technical failure.
- A valid but non-activating market replay remains scientific abstention, not
  `consumed`; gates are not loosened to manufacture a trade.
- The deterministic closed-MTF fixture proves wiring, not market edge or paper
  authority. Fresh passported autonomous evidence is a separate acceptance.
- Historical generations and their receipts remain immutable.
