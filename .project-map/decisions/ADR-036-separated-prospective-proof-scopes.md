# ADR-036: Separate prospective proof scopes without retrospective authority

Status: accepted diagnostic default and separately versioned prospective issuer;
release and actual market evidence require their own acceptance.

## Context

Legacy v2 combines a component/control effect and guided/blinded selector effect.
An equally useful blind candidate does not refute a component improvement.
Neither one contrast nor a caller success flag proves a stronger selector,
council or inherited trait. Current original exposure inventory is incomplete.

## Decision

Keep legacy outcomes and authority unchanged. Add append-only, domain-signed
component/selector/council/inheritance question records bound to named persisted
original owners and exact model/programme snapshots. Actual four-stream data
binding is another immutable record, before outcomes; verified bytes do not
authorize unused data. Public assessments are diagnostic only.

The existing causal owner measures guided/control and blind/control separately;
new scope declarations cannot borrow legacy confirmed labels or credits.
P/PT/PTU/PU original descendant diagnostics derive trait retention and interaction
from exact vectors and hash-verified original products. Interaction-only value
cannot become individual trait credit. No parallel scheduler is introduced.

## Prospective original execution extension

An independent-policy declaration starts a candidate-scoped append-only exposure
capture before all future validation events and original runs/folds. Both real
ingress paths record exact closed CSV sources, warmup/context/holding and source
revision. Historical missing logs are not repaired into completeness. A fresh
question may copy an old discovery recipe, preserving old outcomes as hypothesis
lineage; it must construct new frozen models before the future events.

The named issuer reopens every preregistered original window/arm product,
requires exact-context power, mature trades, fixed external risk ceilings,
non-target safety and a fixed paired-window uncertainty rule. Component,
selector, council and inheritance proof never substitute for one another.
Equal useful guided/blind effects may establish a component, not selector
superiority. Caller assessments remain diagnostic; legacy v2 is unchanged.

Component and descendant native execution reuse the sole arbiter/conversion
kernel, canonical constructor and existing full queue. Whole-matrix preparation
precedes any HTTP replay, then one genuine fenced job publishes each original
arm/checkpoint. The existing Foundry proposes one controlled U after component
confirmation; later unused windows and fresh four-model capture precede child
evaluation. No science retry is created by operational redelivery.

Scope-specific credit/cartridge handoff is idempotent and remains research-only.
It grants neither global Parent nor paper/live permission. All scoped study
projections are excluded from ordinary selection to prevent holdout leakage.

## External evidence and delivery boundaries

No global historical absence claim, actual future window or market authority is
created by software fixtures or planning. 2026 stays paper-only. Future physical
data must mature and be server-authorized; a child needs later independent
events, not the component's observed validation history.
Release into an active sealed generation is prohibited; delivery must wait for
its safe terminal/drained boundary with new loaded-source attestation.
