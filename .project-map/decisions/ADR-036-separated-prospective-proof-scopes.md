# ADR-036: Separate prospective proof scopes without retrospective authority

Status: accepted diagnostic implementation; independent issuance is not delivered.

## Context

Legacy v2 combines a component/control effect and guided/blinded selector effect.
An equally useful blind candidate does not refute a component improvement.
Neither one contrast nor a caller success flag proves a stronger selector,
council or inherited trait. Current original exposure inventory is incomplete.

## Decision

Keep legacy outcomes and authority unchanged. Add append-only, domain-signed
component/selector/council/inheritance question records bound to named persisted
original owners and exact model/programme snapshots. Actual four-stream data
binding is another immutable record, before outcomes; verified bytes do not
authorize unused data. Public assessments are diagnostic only.

The existing causal owner measures guided/control and blind/control separately;
new scope declarations cannot borrow legacy confirmed labels or credits.
P/PT/PTU/PU original descendant diagnostics derive trait retention and interaction
from exact vectors and hash-verified original products. Interaction-only value
cannot become individual trait credit. No parallel scheduler is introduced.

## Remaining boundaries

The complete immutable original training/selection inventory, independent
scope-specific issuer/credit handoff, equal-budget multi-question selector
producer and native four-arm descendant dispatcher are not implemented by this
registry. No actual future window or authority is created. 2026 stays paper-only.
Release into an active sealed generation is prohibited; delivery must wait for
its safe terminal/drained boundary with new loaded-source attestation.
