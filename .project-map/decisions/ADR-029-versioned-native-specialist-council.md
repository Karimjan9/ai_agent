# ADR-029 - Versioned native specialist council

## Decision

Keep separate scalp/hour/day/swing specialists and typed support products.
Council capital/risk allocation operates on one chronological account and one
paper execution owner, not a winner-take-all vote or summed solo backtests.

Seal native member/component versions and horizon prerequisites in stored
passports. Bind explicitly declared research candidates to that original
version through the existing lab dispatcher and immutable evidence chain.
Preregister comparable champion, solo, component/member ablation and retention
arms. Only a separate evaluator consuming original authorized independent
evidence can advance qualification; normal E3/E4/global risk still owns paper.

Adoption and rollback affect future entries. Orders retain their original
owner, strategy/tactic/management versions and request snapshot until close.
Atomic integer shared reservations prevent parallel specialists from spending
the same capital. Unsupported netting/fill evidence remains an explicit
dependency rather than simulated independent broker positions.

Registered typed AST/CALL proposals may enter bounded research before Academy
transfer proof. A separate decision contract consumes actual closed inputs for
confirmation, risk reduction or exit; benchmark vectors never become trading
inputs. This removes the cold-start admission cycle without issuing credit.

## Consequences and unchanged constraints

The existing arbiter remains the sole autonomous research selector; no second
scheduler, automatic Arena or live-order authority is introduced. Historical
research stays pre-2026 and paper events keep their permitted use/maturity.
Numeric transport copies are checked, not blindly trusted. Synthetic tests
prove account and decision wiring only; independent economic gains, retained
inheritance and qualified active specialists require real original evidence.

See `docs/architecture/specialist-council.md` for owner/API boundaries and the
focused end-to-end acceptance contract.
