# ADR-028: Evidence-bounded research meta-learning

Status: accepted implementation architecture; deployment is separately sealed.
Date: 2026-10-04

## Decision

Extend existing memory, Academy, Foundry and arbiter owners rather than create
ten autonomous services. Reuse the append-only research knowledge journal for
prospective prediction/calibration, competing explanations, rejection audits,
measurement questions and finite declarative policy versions. Add scoped
Foundry tables only for typed abstractions and original behavioral observations.

New task seals and actual original artifacts are required. Old observed trials
cannot acquire retrospective predictions or unearned progress. Empty/missing
metrics stay unknown, technical and underpowered outcomes stay separate from
harmful skills, and research scope never automatically confers causal/economic
authority. Immutable hash/identity verification is repeated at consumption.

CALL expansion must preserve the primitive typed program, with library costs
included in compression. Novel task semantics, interpreter resources and real
search efficiency are separate proof levels. Finite research policy challengers
cannot modify the evaluator, budgets, golden labels or scientific acceptance.

## Consequences

The canonical queue/arbiter remains the only execution owner. Recommendations
may choose among already-ready compatible questions; they cannot manufacture
independence, executor admission or paper/live authority. Existing frozen
cohorts remain byte-identical. Safe release requires drain, source sealing,
additive migrations and loaded-worker hash verification.

Navigation: `docs/operations/research-meta-learning.md` lists the ten concrete
capabilities, owners, native hooks and their distinct runtime acceptance.
