# ADR-033: Native depth continuation and original SOLO selection

Date: 2026-10-09
Status: Accepted implementation contract; deployment and market proof separate.

## Decision

Reuse the canonical constructor, original artifact owners, arbiter and dispatcher.
Do not add a parallel scheduler or promote a retrospective witness.

Native reachability depth is a distinct six-slot research purpose. The physical
15000+512 source contract stays intact; sealed bounded execution views own their
actual clocks. The cheap original producer records quote-known opportunities
and actual gate reachability. Original whole-pool and seeded capped rejection
sample are immutable before exactly one original deeper carrier is eligible.
The arbiter's generation-fenced command and typed dispatch outcome preserve
retry identity. Empty/unpowered pools and technical products close neutrally;
no economic learning, independent evidence or trading authority is produced.

Best-qualified SOLO uses three prospective stages: original standalone scoped
qualification, server-owned as-of roster/ranking, then separate final evaluation.
The original programme/passport/horizon/account/cost/external risk are retained;
only explicitly declared full-account source allocation becomes 1.0. Qualification
does not weaken whole-council or paper criteria. Equal-return ranking requires a
preregistered floor. The server refuses caller shortlists, flags and unqualified
fallbacks. An append-only selection record binds original source runs, criteria
and ranking to the final request.

Physical exposure includes all actual inputs/warmup plus a conservative holding
fence. Unknown auxiliary input is refused. Selection-use projection occurs only
after every original panel run completes. Exact self-projection exclusions cannot
erase foreign event use; final validation cannot reuse the selection observations.

## Consequences

Synthetic original producer/consumer execution proves software wiring only.
Same-data deeper observation is not independent evidence. Original matched or
chosen-source SOLO is not retroactively qualified. Market capability, retained
descendant value, prospective paper and broker qualification remain separate.

Six monthly 2027 draft collection windows are read-only planning, with no dataset,
authorization or executable job. 2026 stays paper-only; real untouched data and
pre-outcome authorization are required before dispatch.
