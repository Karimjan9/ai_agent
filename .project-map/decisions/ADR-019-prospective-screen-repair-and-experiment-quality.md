# ADR-019: Prospective screen repair and explicit experiment quality

Status: accepted

## Context

G242 agent 3113 changed `transition_wait_candles` 4 to 5 and closed three trades.
The old control also used a different instrument surface. Screening selection
and confounded nuisance policies cannot identify a causal improvement.

## Decision

The existing arbiter may purchase one fresh `prospective_screen_repair` triplet
per immutable, exact-baseline, single-gene source pair. Source observations are
hypotheses only. Before replay, unchanged control, repair-guided and memory-blinded
arms receive one baseline, passport, exact phase, instrument treatment surface,
cost model and bounded 15,000-row training budget. Ordinary frozen controls also
resolve their unique candidate's instrument gene surface; ambiguous ownership
blocks assignment instead of silently selecting a different router.

Screening assesses immutable observed quotes/MTF, context opportunity count and executable
behavior before expensive confirmation. Data missing, underpowered, no effect and
ready for independent validation remain different results. Missing inputs and
power are episodic uncertainty, not negative skill. The existing conversion kernel
closes discovery idempotently with one blocked powered-opportunity/validation
work item or terminal no-effect receipt. Discovery grants no skill, paper or
reproductive authority. The confirmation service and credit bridge explicitly
reject discovery callbacks; later independent proof is a separate canonical
validation contract, never a relabelled screening result. Ready durable work
outranks a fresh promising-screen proposal.

The source hash also seals the exact declared candidate/control semantic group.
Genetic specialist role and envelope remain separate from the instrument's
canonical decision context. The memory-blinded selector uses the same baseline
Edge Genesis legal-gene firewall as construction; it gains no lesson ranking.
Bounded replacement experiments require terminal technical arms, an invalid
counterfactual contract, a changed source seal and no scientific result. The
first two repairs require zero evaluation runs and distinct constructor causes;
the exceptional final transport repair has the tighter no-result conditions
below. Every replacement records its prior ID and never overwrites evidence.

2026-09-30 amendment: G245 failed semantic-parent construction and G246 failed
unrelated 20-seat construction, both without replay. The exact three-arm G247
was built but its frozen control received only a 780s generic differential
screen timeout; no decision/trade receipt exists. A v2 evaluator therefore
has a distinct bounded 15k budget (1620s default, 1680s ceiling) below the
1800s batch transport and 2400s queue lease. One final fourth attempt is
permitted only for this exact unobserved timeout lineage with a new source
seal. A completed scientific result, a different timeout, or a fourth failure
closes the source. G245-G247 evidence is not rewritten or combined.

2026-09-30 second amendment: G248's three 15k screening arms completed under
one source/data hash with 49/47/44 trades, but none passed screening. Its
immutable result omitted the exact specialist scope receipt; zero reported
context opportunities therefore cannot prove a rare phase. The discovery
remains provisional with a blocked follow-up and no credit, and is not
retroactively relabeled. New prospective arms must execute the source's sealed
context in Python and return a matching receipt. Market-eligible candles are
counted separately from accepted strategy signals; missing scope is
`data_missing`. During G248 an active replay was also mistaken for unchanged
settlement/queue retry storm; bounded active-run waiting and mutex-release
classification prevent that false halt without weakening stale-job checks.

Validation is reserved before discovery using the existing authorized independent
research policy. Already searched history is not relabeled as untouched data and
2026 remains paper-only. Without an authorized unused research window, work stays
non-executable. Existing properly preregistered historical confirmation remains
available; this source does not acquire that authority by replaying its screen.

Reports separate independent control improvement, diagnostic memory/blinded
comparison, retained ablation-proven benefit and knowledge per worker elapsed
hour. Worker elapsed is explicitly a proxy. New causal folds measure thread CPU
and replay wall time, excluding shared feature/audit overhead. Missing legacy
measurements stay unknown; equal resource caps do not imply equal observed CPU.

## Consequences

Existing credit, window, risk, paper and descendant gates remain unchanged. Old
generations and immutable receipts remain intact. Active sealed work drains before
source deployment and worker reload. Fixtures prove wiring, not market edge;
positive causal skill and retained benefit still require real independent evidence.
