# ADR-040: Separate instrument methods from agent selection learning

## Operating-knowledge extension (2026-10-14)

Base skill means correct declared calculation, interpretation, safe use and
refusal; it does not require positive PnL. The existing curated owner publishes
all-26 source-scoped operating lessons and exact installed-recipe teaching packs.
Media review scope is explicit (text, bounded transcript, author excerpt or
metadata-only), never assumed from a link. Known lab conventions, physical
consumer differences and unavailable observations stay visible.

Only a new eligible unused-child generation handoff may bind a code-owned pack
before its own policy seal. Teaching remains a distinct provenance component,
not earned-agent or trading authority. Its selected recipes are revalidated;
old handoffs without a pack remain valid under their original owner. Actual
practice and its contextual preferences use the existing immutable outcomes,
not tutorial exercise labels. No extra scheduler, corpus-driven automatic order
path, active model rebinding or risk/epoch relaxation is introduced.

Date: 2026-10-13

## Context

An instrument implementation, its external-use hypothesis and the specialist's
ability to choose it are not the same skill. Updating a library cannot certify
all its consumers. One successful selector cannot certify every method.

## Decision

Preserve the existing 26-card/five-group inventory. Add append-only source,
method and recipe records, with intrinsic library snapshots. Compile bounded
runtime exports separately for an exact pre-2026 data/execution/baseline/context.
Keep agent policy revisions and original experience in separate tables. Only
original eligibility-verified run artifacts create experience; copied receipts
or caller qualification flags do not. Proposals and fresh rebased candidates
are research-only with reverified original lineage.

Use the existing ordinary/native executors and immutable learning projection,
not a second scheduler. Runtime methods can veto, reduce existing risk or request
an owned exit. Unsupported stateful semantics and construction parameter deltas
remain explicit dependencies, not silently simulated capabilities.

Freeze library and policy axes independently in a four-arm study. Exact account,
costs, risk, source clock, model identity and caps must match. Local archive
execution is diagnostic; confirmation, Master, inherited economics, council
qualification and paper admission remain separately evaluated.

## Consequences

Every card has a use contract, but registry membership is not algorithm or
profitability coverage. Additional sourced methods can be registered without
changing active versions. Automatic copyrighted caption retrieval is not
assumed; supplied permitted transcripts and bounded public documents are
references, not trusted code. All 2026 and later data remains paper-only.
The programme-key index becomes nonunique by AST while the original unique
programme key remains, allowing fresh bound research exports. Rollback refuses
duplicate semantic scopes instead of deleting research history.

Standalone fresh candidate creation alone is not autonomous canonical dispatch.
The explicit method-study work type uses the existing arbiter, constructor and
fenced scoped-arm job, with a four-request atomic preparation barrier and one
original attempt per arm. Its final observations feed only model-owned method
experience and bounded proposals, never ordinary economic learning fan-out.
New ordinary generations may pin approved curated recipes and attach a rederived
own policy to an eligible unused child before seals. Protected exact/comparative,
passport and council owners remain unchanged; they require their own declared
treatment. This opt-in is not evidence that every ordinary seat consumes methods.
The active deployment remains protected until separately verified release,
migrations, actual loaded-worker hashes and canonical admission are complete.

## Closed inputs and original-outcome model extension

The append-only v2 catalog adds closed OHLC proxy and mature-owned-journal
methods without editing v1. Method-only feature capability is non-wire; copied
input fields cannot enable it. Missing quote/volume/model facts remain typed
dependencies, not inferred observations.

An original-outcome finite model is an independently signed numeric asset with
fixed bins, source roster and seen chronological calibration, not a copied
generic performance score or independent certificate. Target-specific attachment
precedes policy/runtime seals; original training and actual physical evaluation
identities are separate. Cash/risk numeric wire normalization is explicit while
hard limits and other ports remain exact. Native shared-account reuse and silent
candidate inheritance refuse until their physical transfer is owned explicitly.
Own-policy comparison rebases four fresh signed carriers from one original
proposal root through the existing canonical study dispatcher; unchanged
selectors close no_effect without compute. Old originals remain immutable.

## Own outcome advice and bounded original execution

Add a separate append-only owned-outcome v2 namespace: an accepted entry must
link to the same original agent/method and physical position, whose uncensored
mature cost-inclusive result reconciles with the complete account. Alias replays
do not increase sample counts; conflicting episode content refuses. Five unique
mature episodes per exact context and positive mean net cash may rank a recipe
for research, not prove causal power. WAIT/veto utility remains unknown, and
multi-recipe positions remain bundle observations. V1 experience/proposals are
preserved; explicitly v2-owned parents cannot downgrade invalid proof to v1.
Only an unused prospective child receives a new server-derived own policy.

The canonical original-study owner may privately sign deadline v2 for future
simple incremental arms with >300, <=600-second caps and <=4096 exact raw rows.
Python reopens source bytes and verifies scope, physical/runtime controls, method
and release before choosing that budget. It does not rewrite an old 300-second
request, authorize full/native/independent/paper work or grant another attempt.

Historical advice retains the same exclusion predicates inside one validated
repeatable-read transaction, ephemeral positive rosters and frozen horizons.
Exact source membership/exclusion and light original run tuples fence advisory
cache revisions; targeted own-transaction retags refuse and caches publish after
commit. Unsupported isolation, nested production transactions or the 50000-ID
roster budget remain operational dependencies, not scientific negatives.
The owned-outcome migration owns its new table only and refuses nonempty rollback;
nullable unassessed model registration remains absence of assessment, not proof.
