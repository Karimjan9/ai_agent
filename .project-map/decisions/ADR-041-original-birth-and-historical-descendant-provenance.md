# ADR-041: Original birth and historical descendant provenance

Date: 2026-10-19
Status: Accepted; bounded software integration verified

ADR-038 separates historical event time from the actual registration clock.
It does not establish that an already selected or trained model has never seen
the proposed validation events. An absent request, a new dataset name, a caller
completeness flag or a new model identifier cannot establish that fact.

## Decision

The existing exposure-inventory owner may create one finite, server-owned cold
component recipe in an unused three-slot source cohort. It creates the models
itself from canonical defaults and fixed alternatives, without parent, training
or memory inputs, and seals the actual birth, selection, schema and source
identities before capture. This is a component question, not evidence that a
memory-guided selector is better than a blind selector. Existing selected models
cannot be retrospectively converted to this origin.

Historical assessment reopens that origin and original physical request/fold
inputs, including failed and partial attempts. Missing original custody,
unproven market identity, physical overlap and source/selection drift remain
dependencies. The global reference-only audit stays exclusion-only. A valid
origin neither authorizes a window nor issues an economic certificate.

The descendant path carries the explicit historical-first-exposure contract
through its proposal, original model creation and registration. Its new
P/P+T/P+T+U/P+U windows must follow the parent validation and holding fence while
ending before 2026. Original ancestry must be proved by the canonical producer;
the cold component origin cannot be copied onto inherited or caller-created
models. Legacy records and their time policies are unchanged.

New preparation and execution remain owned by the existing constructor,
conversion kernel and research arbiter. A source-only construction disposition
is neutral, not a replay result. PAUSE/STOP and locked admission remain binding.
There is no new background scheduler or manual success flag.

Derived-child preparation and six-window readiness may reuse one internally
derived parent proof only within a lexical operation on the existing owner.
Entry and successful exit each reopen the full original proof. Any changed
immutable prefix refuses the result; the context and temporary owner binding
are cleared in `finally`. Locked preparation opens a fresh operation and checks
its current lease/control again after the fresh exit proof. There is no durable
cache, caller-supplied authority object or reuse on the next invocation.

## Consequences

- Source/runtime, physical data, exposure history, statistical utility and
  authority remain separate proofs.
- All events from 2026 onward remain paper-only; an unavailable historical
  window cannot be replaced with a later paper year.
- Synthetic fixtures can prove the software path but cannot supply a market
  advantage, independent production data, Master or Champion qualification.
- Constructor-stage diagnostics are operational observations, not heartbeat
  renewal, scientific progress, permission to retry or evidence of unused data.
