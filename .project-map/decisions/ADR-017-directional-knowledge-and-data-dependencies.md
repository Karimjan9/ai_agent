# ADR-017: Learn exact interventions without inventing missing evidence

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-28

## Context

Repeated losses in one archive can become gene-wide harmful lessons even when
the opposite mutation was never tested. Pre-entry scope vetoes can also hide
raw strategy opportunities behind a zero-signal result. Missing observed
liquidity and unavailable independent research data are external dependencies,
not parameters to relax until a trade appears.

## Decision

Directional learning v2 groups exact gene, old/new values, direction, context,
source and verified frozen-control evidence. Harmful authority needs three
distinct authorized non-overlapping negative windows, no positive window and
original settlement revalidation. Retrieval blocks that exact delta/context,
not the entire gene. Legacy lessons remain history/hypotheses, not blanket bans.

The specialist gate retains upstream signals and emits first-veto/all-predicate
counts at decision time. Composition retains those rejected opportunities.
Missing measured spread/ATR is an explicit liquidity-data reason; execution
cost assumptions cannot impersonate an observed spread. The proof frontier
defers mutation-only activation work when observed liquidity is the missing
prerequisite. Research-window readiness reports unavailable/future data and
continues to exclude the 2026 paper-only epoch.

Existing durable three-arm causal jobs preregister equal fold/row/time caps
and settle a diagnostic comparison only from a complete hash-valid fold set.
The benchmark records guided/blinded/control outcomes idempotently, but does
not claim equal measured CPU, confirmed compounding or authority. Independent
confirmation and descendant consumption remain separate earned evidence.

## Consequences

- Negative knowledge narrows only what was actually tested.
- A blocked data dependency cannot masquerade as strategic failure or credit.
- Tests prove wiring; neither tests nor a diagnostic benchmark manufacture a
  market edge, Research Mentor, Economic Parent or inheritance credit.
