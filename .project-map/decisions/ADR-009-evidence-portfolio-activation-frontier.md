# ADR-009: Research-only activation factorial precedes economic evidence

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-26

## Context

Frozen composition bindings can be valid while strategy signals never cross
the tactic gate. A cold-start council that reserves economic factorial work
only after a positive stepping stone cannot cheaply test whether two legal
changes jointly open that path. Treating a four-arm activation observation as
economic or individual component credit would be a false causal claim.

## Decision

The existing council may replace two repair pairs with one bounded four-arm
`activation_factorial` only when a terminal source has immutable screening
evidence, a valid composition trace, sufficient signal opportunities, a
compatible passport and two distinct legal changes. Keep incumbent and
unconfirmed challenger evidence axes separate; a heuristic priority is not a
calibrated success probability. Freeze a source parameter hash and one common
manifest. C is the exact source vector; A and B each change one gene; AB
changes both. Admission recomputes each executable hash and fails closed on
identity drift.

Replay emits arm-specific decision IDs and candle-shared paired-context IDs,
including a compact no-signal opportunity-universe digest. Settlement requires
immutable response parity, MTF/data/execution/context equality and sufficient
predeclared opportunities. A joint activation is a behavioral hypothesis only.
The conversion kernel records one idempotent semantic receipt and a blocked
fresh-window validation work item; this path never calls component/economic
credit or grants trading authority. A discovery hypothesis may consume at
most one trial on the same frozen data identity.

## Consequences

- Four seats are one paired experiment, not four independent replications.
- Technical mismatch is invalid; low opportunity is underpowered; a local
  no-activation observation is not a universal impossibility proof.
- Economic superiority, independent validation, component credit and later
  consumption require separate future evidence and remain unproven here.
- Historical generation evidence is not rewritten.
